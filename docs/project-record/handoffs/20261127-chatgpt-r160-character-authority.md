## Three distinct sources

- Hero progression: AXE_LEVEL_CAP=155; Character Vault validation permits that level, but browser-origin data is not authoritative earned progression. R151–154 schema and concurrency gaps remain.
- Legacy duel: duelSystem/entry.ts duelStatsFor reads account AvatarProgression through combatProfile.ts and avatarLevel from combatStats.ts, account cap 50, then derives par hero. Missing/ambiguous rows fall back to level 1. Do not change separate account cap or replace it with client save values.
- Dedicated standardized preview: game3dCombatTicket/entry.ts requires character binding, preflight, ledger-only and review flags for baseline (character binding also requires archive). duelCharacterProvenance.mjs checks owner, character, unique row, revision, fingerprint and flags; not earned level/equipment. duelBaselineLoadout.mjs derives fixed level-1 baseline, no equipment, no rewards. Ticket legacy snapshots explicitly not ranked proof. Preserve current disabled/private flags.
- Private PostgreSQL trustedCombatSheet143.mjs derives level from committed XP bound to sealed seed revision, not browser. No equipment/mastery or ranked authorization. R158 private cap candidate separate; on pinned main private progression cap still 100. Its XP formula differs from live hero. It is not a ready PvP adapter.

## Decision gate

Do not silently unify hero and account progression. Owner must choose whether future PvP remains standardized, uses a server-verified character-level-only ruleset, or eventually includes verified gear and mastery. Recommend retaining existing private standardized mode until the authoritative earned-event and equipment ledgers, identity, migration and epoch/revision binding are proven. Keep rewards/ranking disabled; never elevate a fingerprint to earned authority. No change to voluntary world-PvP rules.
