## Interior additions
262 shallow surface detail instances across all four buildings and all three tiers. Armory: 81 (equipment inventory panels, locker door faces, vents, handles). Reactor: 64 (coolant/power gauge boards, bus details and cabinet end cap). Medical Bay: 57 (medical cross, vital status graphics, diagnostic panels and cabinet faces). Barracks: 60 (readiness boards, personnel roster graphics, locker faces). All themed status displays are decorative; no new ability, pickup, interactable or equipment system. Reuse original materials and map detailBox/flushDetails pipeline; no new renderer, dynamic lights, textures, shadow-casting details or animation loop. Surface relief is at most 0.04 m; keep ventilation side-wall areas untouched and avoid the Medical Bay recovery pod being relocated in Stage 2. New details are visual overlays on existing colliders, not independent physical cover. Existing furnishing/cover positions stay unchanged. No new floor-standing furniture, to avoid worsening known traversal issues.
