# Duelist Adventure: the owner's kit fixes (2026-11-22)

The owner listed abilities that did not work as intended, asked for a double jump with air dashes and a taunt on T,
and asked for better animations, effects and sounds (ElevenLabs). This file covers the rules, which come first. The
effects, sounds and new animations follow on the same branch, `claude/duelist-adventure-20261122`.

The owner also asked for another game's source code to be used for the combat. As before (2026-11-20 and
2026-11-21), that was declined. These are the game's own rules, written from the owner's descriptions.

## Lock-on and targets

- The lock key takes whoever is nearest the camera's aim within 20 m. Angle off the aim counts most, then distance.
  - Enemies for everyone.
  - Allies too for a Healer.
- The lock rides the input as `tg` (PROTOCOL 13).
- A targeted ability takes the locked fighter when it qualifies: the right side, alive, in reach, in sight and not
  hidden by a cloak.
- Without a lock:
  - an enemy ability takes the nearest enemy in its arc;
  - Mend heals the Healer herself.
- Levitate and Mind Dominance are refused, not spent, when there is nobody to take them (`abilityRefused`,
  `no-target`).

## Movement

- **Jump:** 2.6 m. The 4 m jump from 809cb1a7 put the head through the 4.2 m ceilings of the story rooms, which now
  cap the feet at 2.2 m (`ROOM_HEADROOM`).
- **Air dash:** Dash in the air dashes 7 m, as far as a roll, with no stamina or Will. There is one dash for each jump.
- **Double jump:** Jump in the air jumps again, as high as the first, once. It gives another dash, so the sequence is
  jump, dash, jump, dash.
- The double-tap air dash is gone. The controls line under the HUD said "double-tap in air" until the fourth round;
  it now says "in the air too".

## Taunt

T (`taunt`, a client command) plays the class's own taunt (`TAUNTS`: `tauntWarrior`, `tauntDuelist`,
`tauntAssassin`, `tauntHealer`): 2.2 s, a 4 s cooldown, picture and voice only. Moving or acting cuts it short. Each
has an animation of its own (fourth round, below); a fighter with no class folds its arms. The controls line under
the HUD now lists T.

## The Assassin's snap across the map

Her simulated body never moved more than 0.09 m a frame. The hips of two staff clips, however, start well off the
body: `staffB` 1.28 m and `staffLeap` 3.71 m. Every other clip starts within 0.1 m. While those clips played (for
example `staffLight2`, a quick second press), she was drawn far from where she was, and the offset swung round her as
she turned.

`client/motionLibrary.mjs` now moves any clip whose hips start more than `OFF_CENTRE` (0.5 m) away back over the
body, and keeps the clip's own motion. Live, turning while striking now keeps her hips within 0.19 m of her body;
before, it was 1.37-3.71 m.

## Effects and weapons (second round)

`client/abilityFx.mjs` is new. It draws with sprites and floor decals whose textures are drawn on canvases, the
shared particle pool and the bodies' own glow, so it has no shader of its own and draws the same with WebGPU and
WebGL. `client/vfx.mjs` calls it.

| What | Look |
| --- | --- |
| Rage | The roar: a ring of flame bursting off the Warrior, a fire ring across the floor, scorched cracks and a heat shimmer. While it lasts, tongues of flame burn along the body's outline, with embers, a heat glow and a warm glow on the body |
| Sanctuary | The dome is gone. It is a healing mist instead: drifting white and light-blue haze over the floor, motes rising through it and a soft rim. Allies standing in it carry light-blue wisps along their outlines and a faint matching glow |
| Mend | A stream of light from the Healer's hand to whoever she heals, and a bloom on them |
| Levitate | A rune circle opens on the floor under the lifted enemy, then a column of light and circling motes |
| Mind Dominance | A violet crown and a watching eye over the head, violet wisps and a violet glow; the crown shatters when broken |
| Shadow Execution (cloaked) | Smoke where she was and where she comes out, a dark streak between them |
| Execution | A golden tether from the Duelist's hand while the target is drawn in; a gold burst and ring as the body is pushed away |
| Leap Strike, Repulse | Light trails the leap. The landing or the slam cracks the floor, throws a ring of force and dust, and streaks rise under those thrown up |
| Air dash, second jump | An after-image streak; a ring of force kicked off the air |
| Taunt | The class's colour flares off the body |

The weapons (`client/crystalWeapon.mjs` `createClassBlade`, `client/classWeapons.mjs`) each have their own shape:

| Class | Weapon | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Emberbrand | Broad and notched, with a molten core showing down its middle, a heavy crossguard with crystal inlays and a spiked pommel |
| Healer | Lumen | Slender with a white core; a halo of light turns round the blade's base, with three motes on it |
| Duelist | Fangs | Curved twin sabres with a pale edge and knuckle bows |
| Assassin | Veilstaff | A hooked blade at each end of a shaft banded with glowing runes |

Each blade is 680-980 triangles.

The body glows are kept low (0.06-0.16), because the arena is dark: at 0.2 the whole body turned the effect's
colour.

Pictures were taken in the browser pane through a local capture helper. Online, the Execution's tether and the mist's
glow on sheltered allies do not show yet, because the public snapshot carries neither the seize nor the zones.

## Sounds and voices (third round)

`tools/duelist/record-ability-sounds.mjs` records the game's sound effects and taunt lines with ElevenLabs:
- sound effects through the sound-generation endpoint;
- spoken lines through text-to-speech, with the premade voices the comms already use.

The output goes to `public/audio/duelist/sfx/` and is listed in `sfx.json`. The prompts describe sounds in plain
words, never another work's.

```
node --env-file=<a git-ignored .env file holding ELEVENLABS_API_KEY> tools/duelist/record-ability-sounds.mjs [--dry] [--only a,b] [--force]
```

There are 59 recordings, 1.4 MB in all: 51 effects (about 60 s of sound) and 8 spoken lines (166 characters).

- **The blades and the body**, which replace the synthesized basics: swings (light and heavy), hits on the body,
  the Veil and shields, blocks, parries, push, pull, throw, dash, roll, knockdown, the Veil collapsing, defeat.
- **The abilities:**
  - Rage: a roar (a man's or a woman's, by the body), a fire ignition, then a hard-breathing loop and a crackling
    fire loop while the berserk lasts, not through the roar;
  - Leap Strike: a whoosh and a ground slam at the landing;
  - Repulse's slam; Mend's chime; the Sanctuary's mist;
  - Levitate; Mind Dominance and its breaking;
  - Veil Cloak; the cloaked blink;
  - the Execution's pull, stab and shove;
  - the air dash, the second jump, Counter, Quickstep, Empower, Blade Throw.
- **The taunts:** each class has one line, recorded in a man's voice and a woman's.
  - Warrior: "Is that all you have got?"
  - Duelist: "Too slow."
  - Assassin: "You never saw me coming."
  - Healer: "I will still be standing."

`client/audio.mjs` loads `sfx.json` once the sound has started:
- A recording plays where one exists, picking a variant and varying its speed by up to 4%.
- The synthesized sound stays as the fallback, for a sound that has no recording and while recordings load.
- `loop` and `stopLoop` run the Rage loops frame by frame.

`client/director.mjs` gives each new event its sound. An actor's `voice` (`f` for the Huntress and female bodies,
otherwise `m`) picks which roar, breathing and taunt play.

Checked in the browser:
- All 59 files decode. One came out nearly silent (the roll, -50 dB) and was recorded again (-27.6 dB). The woman's
  breathing came out about 10 dB quieter than the man's, so its gain is raised.
- In a match as a Warrior on the Huntress body, these played: `rageRoar_f` and `fireIgnite` at the special; the
  `rageBreath_f` and `fireLoop` loops through the berserk; `taunt_warrior_f` on T.

Nobody has listened to them yet: the pane's sound was muted.

## New animations (fourth round)

Seven clips were made for the game from scratch in Blender. No source clip was used: each is posed key by key on its
weapon's own stance by `tools/duelist/blender/moments.py`. `weapon_clips.py` loads it and puts the clips in
`class-weapons.anim.glb` beside the others. A key sets:
- the trunk's turn, lean and bend, and the hips' shift;
- where each hand is, and which way its thumb or blade points;
- how far the fingers open;
- the head's own turn, nod and tilt;
- each foot's step;
- a tremble.

Every channel runs on a monotone cubic through its keys. The arm and leg solvers are the ones the cuts use, and every
hand reached its mark (no miss above 1 mm, wrists within 42°).

| Clip | Move | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `swordTauntWarrior` | Warrior's taunt | The sword dropped low out to the side, chest out, chin up, the free hand held out palm up and beckoning twice |
| `swordTauntHealer` | Healer's taunt | The blade raised straight up overhead, the free hand on the heart, chin up |
| `bladesTaunt` | Duelist's taunt | The twin blades crossed before the face, then swept out and down to the sides, the head tilted |
| `staffTaunt` | Assassin's taunt | The staff planted upright at her side and leant on, the free hand on her hip, hips cocked, head tilted |
| `swordRoar` | Rage | Hunched and gathered, then the chest thrown up, the arms flung wide at shoulder height and the head thrown back, trembling through the scream, then down into a forward lean to charge (the 0.5 s wind-up; the rest settles) |
| `bladesExecution` | Execution | The free hand thrown out at the enemy and drawn back to the chest as it is pulled in (0-0.4 s), the right blade coiled at the hip, driven straight into the centre with a step in (the right hand's fastest at 0.75 s, the kill at 0.78 s), drawn out, the shove with the left hand (1.18 s) |
| `staffDominate` | Mind Dominance | Two fingers to the temple, then the hand thrown at the enemy, the staff kept in the right |

- Each clip is made to its move's own seconds. `core/moveMotion.mjs` plays it straight through.
- The full Blender build takes about 40 s and leaves the 73 clips made before as they were.
- The motion pack went from 131 to 138 clips (5.55 MB). The pack's other clips are unchanged.
- A new test checks that each class's T plays its own clip, and that the Execution's thrust lands just before the kill.

Rebuild:

```
blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/duelist/blender/weapon_clips.py -- <actionforge_80_clips_fit.glb> <out dir>
    [--moments-only --only a,b --pictures a:7,b:9 --turn 0.6]   # a quick look: one sheet, a row a clip
```

Copy `class-weapons.anim.glb` and `.clips.json` from the out dir to `tools/duelist/source/`, then run
`node tools/duelist/build-motion-pack.mjs tools/duelist/source/actionforge-80.anim.glb`.

Checked:
- in Blender pictures, from the front and the side (the roar from behind too);
- in practice (`/duelist-next/play?mode=practice`), offline, with the bot held still: each class's T, the Rage, the
  Execution and Mind Dominance.

In that practice check:
- the Execution drew the bot in from 4.5 m to 1.07 m by 0.42 s, killed it at 0.78 s and shoved the body out to 6 m;
- Mind Dominance held the bot for its 2 s.

The first roar was too tame from behind, so its arms were raised and spread wider.

The pictures were taken in the browser pane, which was hidden, by stepping the game by hand. The match ignores input
during its start countdown, so presses only count once it is live.
