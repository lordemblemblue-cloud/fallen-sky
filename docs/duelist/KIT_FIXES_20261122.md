# Duelist Adventure: the owner's kit fixes (2026-11-22)

The owner listed abilities that did not work as intended, asked for a double jump with air dashes and a taunt on T,
and asked for better animations, effects and sounds (ElevenLabs). This file covers the rules, which come first. The
effects, sounds and new animations follow on the same branch, `claude/duelist-adventure-20261122`.

The owner also asked for another game's source code to be used for the combat. As before (2026-11-20 and
2026-11-21), that was declined. These are the game's own rules, written from the owner's descriptions.

## Lock-on and targets

- The lock key takes whoever is nearest the camera's aim within 20 m. Angle off the aim counts most, then distance.
  - Enemies for everyone.
  - Allies too for a Healer.
- The lock rides the input as `tg` (PROTOCOL 13).
- A targeted ability takes the locked fighter when it qualifies: the right side, alive, in reach, in sight and not
  hidden by a cloak.
- Without a lock:
  - an enemy ability takes the nearest enemy in its arc;
  - Mend heals the Healer herself.
- Levitate and Mind Dominance are refused, not spent, when there is nobody to take them (`abilityRefused`,
  `no-target`).

## Movement

- **Jump:** 2.6 m. The 4 m jump from 809cb1a7 put the head through the 4.2 m ceilings of the story rooms, which now
  cap the feet at 2.2 m (`ROOM_HEADROOM`).
- **Air dash:** Dash in the air dashes 7 m, as far as a roll, with no stamina or Will. There is one dash for each jump.
- **Double jump:** Jump in the air jumps again, as high as the first, once. It gives another dash, so the sequence is
  jump, dash, jump, dash.
- The double-tap air dash is gone.

## Taunt

T (`taunt`, a client command) plays the class taunt: 2.2 s, a 4 s cooldown, picture and voice only. Moving or
acting cuts it short. Until each class has its own clip, it plays the pack's folded arms.

## The Assassin's snap across the map

Her simulated body never moved more than 0.09 m a frame. The hips of two staff clips, however, start well off the
body: `staffB` 1.28 m and `staffLeap` 3.71 m. Every other clip starts within 0.1 m. While those clips played (for
example `staffLight2`, a quick second press), she was drawn far from where she was, and the offset swung round her as
she turned.

`client/motionLibrary.mjs` now moves any clip whose hips start more than `OFF_CENTRE` (0.5 m) away back over the
body, and keeps the clip's own motion. Live, turning while striking now keeps her hips within 0.19 m of her body;
before, it was 1.37-3.71 m.

## Effects and weapons (second round)

`client/abilityFx.mjs` is new. It draws with sprites and floor decals whose textures are drawn on canvases, the
shared particle pool and the bodies' own glow, so it has no shader of its own and draws the same with WebGPU and
WebGL. `client/vfx.mjs` calls it.

| What | Look |
| --- | --- |
| Rage | The roar: a ring of flame bursting off the Warrior, a fire ring across the floor, scorched cracks and a heat shimmer. While it lasts, tongues of flame burn along the body's outline, with embers, a heat glow and a warm glow on the body |
| Sanctuary | The dome is gone. It is a healing mist instead: drifting white and light-blue haze over the floor, motes rising through it and a soft rim. Allies standing in it carry light-blue wisps along their outlines and a faint matching glow |
| Mend | A stream of light from the Healer's hand to whoever she heals, and a bloom on them |
| Levitate | A rune circle opens on the floor under the lifted enemy, then a column of light and circling motes |
| Mind Dominance | A violet crown and a watching eye over the head, violet wisps and a violet glow; the crown shatters when broken |
| Shadow Execution (cloaked) | Smoke where she was and where she comes out, a dark streak between them |
| Execution | A golden tether from the Duelist's hand while the target is drawn in; a gold burst and ring as the body is pushed away |
| Leap Strike, Repulse | Light trails the leap. The landing or the slam cracks the floor, throws a ring of force and dust, and streaks rise under those thrown up |
| Air dash, second jump | An after-image streak; a ring of force kicked off the air |
| Taunt | The class's colour flares off the body |

The weapons (`client/crystalWeapon.mjs` `createClassBlade`, `client/classWeapons.mjs`) each have their own shape:

| Class | Weapon | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Emberbrand | Broad and notched, with a molten core showing down its middle, a heavy crossguard with crystal inlays and a spiked pommel |
| Healer | Lumen | Slender with a white core; a halo of light turns round the blade's base, with three motes on it |
| Duelist | Fangs | Curved twin sabres with a pale edge and knuckle bows |
| Assassin | Veilstaff | A hooked blade at each end of a shaft banded with glowing runes |

Each blade is 680-980 triangles.

The body glows are kept low (0.06-0.16), because the arena is dark: at 0.2 the whole body turned the effect's
colour.

Pictures were taken in the browser pane through a local capture helper. Online, the Execution's tether and the mist's
glow on sheltered allies do not show yet, because the public snapshot carries neither the seize nor the zones.
