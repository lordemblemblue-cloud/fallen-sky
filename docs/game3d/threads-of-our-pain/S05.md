# S05 — Three Travelers
R56 | Source: R55 original S05 catalog, M06/M14 scripts and S04 lead. Rescue Tome, Henut and Livilla in Wolfveil gate fields; three danger pockets and individual keepsakes. Original counterpart is mixed civilian column under external invasion. New keepsake designs and encounters are proposed.
## Scene 1 — An interrupted journey
Age fifteen. The road curves around a low stone shoulder. A wagon has tipped into the grass; a wooden wheel turns slowly in the breeze. A bundle of grain has spilled across the path. Farther away a voice calls for help, then another answers. The player can approach from the main road, a shallow farm track or a safe ridge path. No timer begins merely because the area streams in. The threat starts locally as the player enters a clearly marked encounter pocket.
Tome (struggling with the wagon's load, embarrassed): “Henut is still by the wall. Don't waste time on my rice.”
Hero: “Can you stand?”
Tome: “If you give me something to lean on. The axle's pinning the sack, not my leg.”
The player stabilizes the wagon at a visible brace point or uses the side access to free his coat. A small hostile creature can be driven away if present; safe interaction occurs only once the immediate pocket is clear. Tome's proposed keepsake is a knotted cloth measure from his household. He checks it after standing, then asks about the others. The player may recover it from the wagon shelf or leave it for Tome to retrieve when safe. No requirement to take his property.
## Scene 2 — The jar and the wall
Henut is at the low retaining wall, holding a wrapped jar against her chest. A damaged section of wall sheds dust and small stones. The direct route is unsafe until the marked gap settles; a longer walk around the low end avoids the hazard. A hostile creature watches from the field, but the bypass allows a noncombat rescue if the player keeps to the protected lane.
Henut (points rather than shouts): “Livilla needs help. She went toward the ditch when the wagon turned.”
Hero: “We'll get there. Give me the jar if you need both hands.”
Henut (hesitates, then loosens her grip): “My sister made it. There's nothing expensive inside.”
Hero: “Then let's keep it whole.”
Player can carry the wrapped jar a short distance or guide Henut while she retains it. A simple recovery interaction returns it at the safe node, and she never loses it due to a failed jump or full inventory. If Tome is already safe, the hero tells her; if not, he does not falsely reassure her. Henut is concerned, observant and unwilling to trade another person's safety for her own.
## Scene 3 — The ditch
Livilla has made a temporary foothold using a broken fence rail. She directs the protagonist toward the stable bank rather than waiting passively. A creature lurks beyond the ditch; the player can distract it away from the protected crossing or defeat it. An alternate farm track leads to the same refuge. Her proposed keepsake is a folded cloth bearing a household stitch, tucked under a loose strap. It is recovered without entering the hazard lane.
Livilla (breathless but decisive): “Don't leave anyone.”
Hero: “There are three of you. I'm checking each road.”
Livilla: “Good. I can walk if you can keep the path clear.”
If rescued first: “Tome went back for the wagon. Henut was near the wall.” If last: “They're both out? Then stop looking at me like I'm going to fall over.” If she is assisted but the keepsake was left, she retrieves it at the refuge after the threat resets. No new faction selection or identical personality across the three travelers.
## Scene 4 — The mixed column
Age eighteen. The same bend and rock shoulder border a route crowded with civilians fleeing external invaders. A defender moves a barrier to let a mixed column through. The older protagonist is asked to escort groups between two marked safe points; their clothing and faces are distinct from the three younger-world travelers. One carries a grain sack, another a wrapped vessel, another a cloth bundle. These are visual rhymes, not proof of shared identities.
Defender: “Don't count the wagons. Count who's made it past the turn.”
Hero: “Is the lower road still open?”
Defender: “For walking. Keep the carts here.”
Player may cover the marked interval or guide the walking group along the protected side path. At the refuge, a civilian reaches back to steady someone else's shoulder. The older hero hears the creak of the younger wagon; the younger, when catching his breath, hears hurried footsteps on a dry road that is presently empty. No mandatory civilian death for failure; retreat and resume from last safe group receipt. This optional counterpart is not M05's authored death or a replay of it.
## Scene 5 — Three names on the list
At the safe gate staging point, Tome offers to share what grain survived, Henut checks her jar and Livilla asks for a place to sit. The warden records each arrival. Rescue order affects immediate greetings, not later access. If all three keepsakes are recovered, the travelers can identify them at S06; if not, owners can bring them themselves or give verbal descriptions. No compulsory collection of personal objects.
Tome: “I thought I'd be carrying that sack for another twenty years.”
Henut: “You still can. Just carry less.”
Livilla (half smile): “Next time, we walk.”
Hero: “Next time, take the road together.”
The three walk toward the fountain when the player is ready; they do not wait in the road to block other quests. Sable can invite the hero to the later parley. If the quest was skipped, M14 still obtains their core reports. Their survival and present relationships do not establish the identity or guilt of a future attacker.
## Quest contract and consequences
S05_SEEN → RESCUED_TOME/HENUT/LIVILLA in any order → SAFE_ARRIVALS_RECORDED → COMPLETE. KEEPSAKE_A/B/C and help method optional, independently durable; return receipts idempotent. No S04 oath prerequisite. Each pocket has safe staging and retry; retreat preserves completed rescues, local danger resets only for incomplete pockets. No global timer, forced civilian loss or rewards repeated on reload. Proposed standard XP and modest travel supplies once; keepsakes are narrative receipts, not sellable loot or permanent combat buffs. S06 available even with no keepsakes; if S05 skipped, the parley uses NPC arrival summaries and no claim of player rescue. Both lives believe they helped strangers; developer truth is shared life with structural echoes. No migration by NPCs, no reincarnation claim, no new main gates.
