## Prompt 02 — Spawn, root bend and forest ground
Construct the initial 15–25 m as a gently winding trail with a dark compacted earth strip, slightly raised shoulders, small stones pressed into soil and intermittent broad fern groups. The path itself stays free of compulsory hops. On the downhill side, expose a short angular rock face with proposed height 0.7–1.3 m and two broad strata, one shallow diagonal crease painted in dark gray and one geometry chip at the upper corner. Moss occupies the damp shaded lower edge and a few protected creases, not the exposed top. Use flat color planes and a shared stone/soil atlas instead of sculpted pebble detail. On the uphill side, place the existing root-bend landmark: angular trunk, root extensions 0.2–0.35 m above ground off the central lane and a worn side of root where travelers pass. A shallow optional vault practice root (proposed maximum 0.25 m high after controller test) has a flat walking bypass. The optional interaction is not a main quest gate. Keep collision separate from decorative thin fern geometry, and preserve ground beneath all visible root gaps. Avoid dense grass clusters or shadows from individual leaves. Record M01_SPAWN and M01_ROOT_BEND with clear recovery space.

## Prompt 03 — River, split boulder and stream loop
Build a curved river bank with a stable dry viewing shelf and a visually recognizable split boulder at M01_BOULDER. Proposed boulder envelope: 2.1 m long, 1.4 m wide, 1.3 m high; two unequal angular masses with a narrow V-shaped upper split tapering before reaching the ground. Use broad polygonal surfaces, one chipped face roughly 0.2 m across and a dark painted shallow crease continuing from the split. A slightly green band of moss sits along the sheltered downstream foot, with darker wet faces below a thin waterline stain; top stays predominantly dry gray. The split must not imply an enterable doorway. Use a silhouette-faithful low-cost LOD and simple convex collision; no required climbing on the boulder. Put the crooked tree on higher bank, slightly inclined away from the main path, broad crown and sparse roots where they do not obscure the boulder silhouette. Shore should transition from brown packed soil to rounded gray pebbles, then muted shallow blue-green water. Use simple flow shading and low-overdraw foam near rocks, not expensive water physics or global reflections. Optional streamside path reconnects ROOT_BEND and RIVER_BEND; if a shallow crossing is added under the existing source proposal, verify supported wading depth (initial visual target 0.1–0.25 m) and a dry bypass. No hidden underwater entrance here: M01 has none in the source. No compulsory swimming, damage or climb. Mark M01_RIVER_BEND and safe return, and retain silhouette under low graphics.

## Prompt 04 — Merchant roadside turnout and vegetation kit
Create the source-proposed 6 × 8 m pullout, slightly off the 3–5 m road. Use packed soil with two dull parallel wheel-wear strips shown by vertex color, scattered noncolliding straw and small pebbles outside the route. Cart uses faceted timber beams, two broad wheels and dark iron fittings, with one worn corner and a clearly visible loose strap. Do not spend geometry on individual nails or fabric threads. Show dents in wood with painted darker planes, broad geometry only at damaged edge visible in silhouette. Merchant and animal occupy fixed safe interaction anchors, leaving the walking lane clear. Reserve enough turn space for camera and NPC animation. Preserve proximity or dialogue warning, optional strap help, neutral/decline choices, animal reaction and road return. Do not add an optional side quest ID or grant permanent gear here. Build tree modules with a small set of angular trunk profiles and broad grouped foliage shapes; vary rotation, scale and tint rather than making every tree unique. Patch moss at shaded tree bases, add ferns near damp low points and sparse ground clumps on shoulder, never obscure interaction prompts. Use instancing and collision only for large trunks and structural rocks. Test all merchant skip/dialogue paths.

## Prompt 05 — Sky window, trail fork and clearing approach
Create the M01 sky window by spacing canopy masses so a minimum roughly 12 m opening is readable from the trail; survey M01_MOON_AZIMUTH and M01_FRAGMENT_TRAJECTORY, without inventing heading. Upper route: gently ascending broad shelf, one proposed weathered cliff wall 2–3 m high with two or three large planar faces, a shallow diagonal fracture, one missing corner and sparse sheltered moss. Main walking lane stays 3–5 m where source suggests; do not require climbing or jumps. Stream route: lower damp path with shallow drainage marks, modest slope and a dry route where crossing is optional. Join both at M01_CLEARING_EDGE, keep homeward loop accessible. Upper overlook has stable edge collision and a safe return ramp; no compulsory glide. If existing gliding is supported, survey landing shelf outside M02 trigger and preload collision. For sky fracture, use restrained silver emissive seam and falling fragment in sky layer, no global strobe or full-screen distortion. Leaves near light may change motion subtly using existing lightweight animation, not simulation of every leaf. Keep warm daylight shifting toward dusk via existing environment; captions and residual glow support players who face away. Trigger actual M01 progression only on verified cue and voluntary arrival, as source specifies. No enemies, traps, relics, new secret tunnel or older-world scene.

Suggested inclined central stone: 2.4 × 1.6 × 0.7 m envelope with one broad tilted face, two uneven chipped edges, a shallow angled crease painted on its rain-darkened surface and a small true silhouette notch. Blue-gray ambient, restrained silver emission, damp soil and sparse moss along shaded outer wall, no per-rock lights. No hidden passage, underwater entrance, loot room, trap or extra NPC.
