## Scene 2 — A thousand years
Spirit (looking past him): “I watched the silver light leave.”
Hero (voice catches): “I remember that light.”
Spirit (turns): “Then why are you still here?”
Hero: “I don't know. What happened to the people?”
Spirit (after silence): “I counted them until I could no longer remember the number. A thousand years, perhaps. That is how long it has felt.”

The thousand years is testimony, not a confirmed calendar. Ask how it counts time: the spirit points to incomplete marks on the plinth. “I stopped making marks when I couldn't tell one day from the next.” Ask about the light: “Warmth. People moving. I couldn't see where they went.” Promise restoration, listen, or insist on entry. If D03 complete, hero may say “Someone else spoke of waiting.” Spirit: “Then you know it isn't an easy thing to do.” Do not settle identity or cosmology. Threatening the figure forms a visible ward; no attack until clear confrontation boundary or choice.

## Scene 3 — Return or defend
Peaceful path: place the offering on its original recess using custody receipt, or verify protected object in place and turn holder to the worn notch. Incorrect orientation simply allows correction. The spirit kneels, resting its hand beside the object.
Spirit (nearly inaudible): “That's where it belongs.”
Hero: “I didn't know.”
Spirit: “You could have kept it.”
Hero: “I could have.”

Combat path: the manifestation blocks the broad center with sweeping arcs and visible circular ward. Proposed three recovery intervals teach spacing; avoid marked arc, strike during recovery, step out of ward. No Celestial Sight or permanent spirit killing implied. Retreat safely through threshold; retry from checkpoint. Defeat disperses the defensive form, not speaking guardian.
Spirit: “I thought you'd come to take the rest.”
Hero (lowers weapon): “I came to find out what happened.”

## Scene 4 — A guard's account

Hero (turns page): “The last group.”
Attendant: “Looking for a name?”
Hero: “A place, I think. I'm not sure.”

Copy the account, leave it in archive or return later. The older senses dry crypt dust while the younger hears a pen scratch paper. No mandatory paired focus; S03 is not D03. Account can supplement M13 without gating it. No confirmed name or identity.

## Scene 5 — Let the room be quiet
Spirit (sits, unclenches hands): “I thought if I waited long enough somebody would tell me where they went.”
Hero: “I wish I could.”
Spirit: “You remembered the light. That's something.”

Choose “I'll remember this place.” / “I have to keep looking.” / silent departure. Replies: “Then it won't be entirely empty.” / “Go while you still have somewhere to go.” / one nod.
