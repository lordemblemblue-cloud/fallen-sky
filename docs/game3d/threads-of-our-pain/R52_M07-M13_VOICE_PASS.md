## M07 — The Mark of a Forgotten Age
### M07.V01 — A mistake worth keeping (after ancient mark inspection)
The historian lowers his lantern so that its light travels sideways across the grooves. The mineral crust casts a narrow shadow. The boy follows the accidental diagonal scratch in the air with his finger but catches himself before contact. Let the player choose to inspect the stone at their own pace; frame the hand and the actual groove together only during a voluntary close look.
Historian (matter-of-fact): “See where the pale layer crosses the cut? Whatever made that mark came first.”
Hero: “Even the little line at the bottom?”
Historian: “Especially that one. A copyist might leave out a mistake.”
Hero (withdraws his hand): “Don't leave it out of your drawing.”
The historian nods and copies the flaw. This establishes why the scratch matters, without giving the player a precise date or making the historian a believer. In the original scene, the authored tool slip must reproduce the same small diagonal mark and remain visible before the runner speaks.
### M07.V02 — A prediction, not a confession (before historian debrief)
On the return walk the boy pauses at the junction and points to a shelf hidden behind a bend. The historian checks his map; he has not yet surveyed the bend with the boy. On the far side, water falls from a split stalactite. Let the player reach the predicted feature before the response.
Historian: “You said there would be a ledge.”
Hero: “I thought there would be a lamp on it, too.”
Historian (looks at bare stone): “There isn't one.”
Hero: “There was.” [He does not elaborate unless the player has chosen the truthful branch.]
Private branch: “I got the ledge right.” Historian: “That much we can record.” Cautious branch: “Maybe I was remembering another cave.” Historian: “Then let's compare them.”
Direction: the boy's confidence briefly breaks when a mundane detail differs. The historian records the route prediction, not a verified lamp. No forced theory. The sketch retains the separate adjacent crest for M08. The archive lead remains available after every branch.

Original lamp is a dressing prop, not an ancient collectible. Test skipped dialogue, all disclosures, hazard bypass, fresh mark event once and readable scratch in low settings. No focus relic.
