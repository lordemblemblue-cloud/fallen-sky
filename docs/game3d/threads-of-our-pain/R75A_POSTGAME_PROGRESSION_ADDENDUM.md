## 1. Progression across both endings
Recommend that both outcomes retain the player's earned character level, experience, allocated stat points, learned ordinary skills, unlocked equipment slots, enhancements, materials, earned currency, cosmetics and legitimate achievement receipts. The player has spent the campaign building one shared existence, even though it has been experienced at two ages. Losing the entire character build merely because the player selected a narrative outcome would discourage an emotionally sincere choice. Both branches awaken or continue as the eighteen-year-old protagonist under R65; this is not the introduction of a new level-one character. Exact level cap, currency rules, stat balancing and multiplayer eligibility remain the responsibility of the existing progression systems, not new rules invented here.

Use one logical ownership record for each genuinely earned item or technique. The older counterpart's already-owned sword, glove or technique shown during an alignment is not an extra economic grant. A younger discovery claim retains its existing unique receipt; its counterpart animation is a story representation. If an item has legitimately been sold, consumed or dismantled before commitment, do not resurrect it from a historical discovery record. If a piece of equipment no longer fits the active avatar or is not supported by the destination, keep it safely in owned storage and explain why it cannot currently be equipped. Recalculate derived stats from actual equipped items and earned progression; do not add two character sheets or silently grant missing equipment. Preserve existing enhancement and socket rules.

Timeline-specific delivery parcels, temporary keys, mission-bound tokens and unfinished quest supplies should be retained as archival records or locked in their originating save context, not converted into spendable goods. Ordinary earned equipment remains distinct from borrowed quest props. Any exceptional restriction must be disclosed before ending confirmation and approved in a specific item table. No mandatory equipment destruction, reset fee, superior-ending loot or invented migration ability.

## 2. Two outcomes, two exploration contracts
Sacrifice: recommend a modest freely explorable restored region after M20's awakening and ancient-history revelation, with safe paths, public facilities and independently authored living residents. The full restored-world scope remains undecided. Former age-fifteen acquaintances are historical/deceased; their homes, names and traces can provide quiet context, not living quest contacts. The future burial/spirit-recovery/possible permanent migration campaign remains an expansion proposal, not an automatic portal or a reward for finishing the base story. The restored world's exact relation to the older third-reality concept and its shrine tradition remains unresolved; do not silently merge their histories.

Refusal: continued exploration is owner-confirmed. Recommend that the initial playable postgame begins as the eighteen-year-old in the immediate fused recovery phase, while the roughly twenty-year coda remains a clearly labeled future cinematic until the owner determines whether later-phase gameplay is desired. This avoids an accidental playable age of thirty-eight or a sudden disappearance of people whose fates have not been authored. The preserved landforms carry original damage; only surveyed open routes, actual survivors and completed repairs may be presented. Reconstruction may open new paths later, but no new quest IDs are added in R75. A previous shelter, guardian or convoy encounter cannot automatically be replayed as current history.

Both branches use the same earned campaign-completion recognition. Do not rank one ending as morally superior, or hide a better character build behind sacrifice or refusal. The two choices should differ in lived history, scenery, available people and future story possibilities, not automatic economic advantage.

## 3. The pre-finale archive and replay proposal
Recommend an immutable pre-choice snapshot created at R65 SAFE_REVIEW after the immediate confrontation is resolved and before any ending writes. Record actual campaign state, local phase, discovered facts, quest and focus flags, item ownership, character build, active checkpoint and source schema revision. Make a playable copy of that snapshot only if the owner approves isolated replay. The completed campaign remains the primary active save; a replay is explicitly a separate historical playthrough, not a canonical ability to travel backward. Players may explore valid missed opportunities within that copy, including original scenes only when its actual authored schedule permits. A missed phase is not made available merely because a snapshot exists. Earlier chapter revisit requires a separately supported checkpoint and cannot manufacture evidence.

Recommend strict economic isolation by default: no replay currency, materials, gear, experience, achievements or quest receipts write into the completed save. A later account-level cosmetic or completion policy can be approved separately with explicit unique ownership rules. Gallery-only viewing is always read-only. No reset of the committed ending, no duplicate rewards for viewing an old cinematic. If storage or the destination fails, retain the original safe checkpoint and do not silently overwrite the archive. Before confirmation, show whether snapshot creation succeeded; if it failed, offer retry or cancel rather than claiming protection. Existing guest entry remains unchanged; do not require a new account system. Server-owned rewards remain authoritative and isolated; client save data cannot grant privileged rewards or impersonate another player. Exact storage mechanism, export support, backup quotas and recovery behavior need engineering verification.

## 4. Journal and ending confirmation
Before committing, present a neutral summary of what changes: playable age, known population consequences, actual postgame scope, retained progression, restricted mission items, and available replay policy. Show only genuinely detected incomplete or closing opportunities, not a demand to finish all 105 optional entries. Offer return to exploration and explicit confirmation separately; a dialogue skip or repeated click is never consent. Final confirmation remains blocked in a shipping build until approved consequences and save behavior exist.

Journal records should distinguish available, waiting for a valid visit, observed, relic recovered with recollection remaining, completed, unavailable in this timeline, and historical record. A recovered D01 sword cannot be granted again by S43; D25/D45 second vistas remain separate from claim. S27 verified receipt may support S29 without falsely completing the pump; S55 partial gardens keep their local state. Historical entries retain actual evidence and consent boundaries. A new-world NPC or ordinary garden is not an automatic replacement for a missed older-world action.
