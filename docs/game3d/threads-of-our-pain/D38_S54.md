## Scene 1 — The space after the last word

Age fifteen. Behind a quiet sanctuary terrace, the hero finds a writing shelf under a partly broken lintel. The stone is worn smooth at elbow height. A narrow wall crack resembles a descending stroke in a handwritten name. Under a protected glassless wooden cover is a scrap with old ink; the script uses the same uneven angle the hero uses when his wrist is tired. A keeper has tied the cover to the shelf to stop wind from lifting it. The hero studies the page without moving it.

Keeper: “We found it when we cleared the ledge. We don't know who left it.”

Hero: “That last line looks wrong.”

Keeper: “Wrong how?”

Hero (touching his own wrist): “I stop there when the pen catches.”

The keeper gives permission to examine the paper. The player may read the transcription, look at the ink shapes or leave. The inscription isn't the account of a divine encounter; it is a plain request to leave a lantern ready near the shelter. The name remains partially obscured. The handwriting recognition is a personal observation, not conclusive historical attribution. From a broad stationary shelf, the ink stroke on the cover, a crack on the wall and distant lintel align into a distinctive signature-like silhouette. # S54 — The Two Invitations

## Scene 1 — A place to leave a message

The keeper notices the hero studying the sheltered shelf. “If you're waiting for someone, we leave messages here. People come and go.” The hero sees a note whose handwritten final stroke resembles his own. Hero (15): “I know this handwriting.” Keeper: “Then you may know who it's for.” Hero: “I don't know if I do.” The note asks a traveler to leave a light near the shelter. The player can examine it, ask the keeper to protect it, or leave without responding. Declining preserves future availability. No threat or urgency timer. If D38 done, the hero recognizes the quill stroke; otherwise the ordinary note is enough. Never force entry into D38 or reward the quill here.

At the writing shelf the player can select a response: practical — “I left the light where you said.” Curious — “Who are you waiting for?” Personal — “There is room beside it.” Or choose to leave only an unsigned blank card indicating willingness to hear again. All are optional. Hero: “I'll leave it here.” If the player declines, keeper says “It can wait.” The record tracks actual selected text only; no fake completion or inferred relationship. Paper and ink supplied by the keeper; no fetch or payment. The keeper remains a local NPC, not a supernatural messenger. On exit the player can return later to inspect the holder.

If a reply is already selected in the younger perspective, the folded card bears that exact line but the older may only see it after the authored correspondence flags match. If no younger reply exists, the older sees his own ordinary invitation and may inspect or leave it; do not fabricate an answer.
