## 4. Shared material and detail rules
Stone/soil: opaque broad faceted planes, dry gray tops, darker rain/waterline and sheltered moss, atlas or vertex color for hairlines; real geometry only for visible chips, silhouette and structural floor. Timber/cloth: visible beam/strap/wheel shape, painted scratches, broad folds, no individual nails/threads. Foliage: broad instanced crown and sparse fern groups, varied scale/rotation/tint, no individual leaf shadows, no opaque shrub blocking cues. Water: visual surface and actual floor/water volume distinct; no mandatory crossing, reflections or swimming. Lunar cue: warm normal dusk becomes local blue-gray/silver, stable shape and captions at Low, no strobe or expensive full-screen distortion. One active realm. Shared directional light and limited emissions, no per-rock lights or dense particles. R87 ordinary distance LOD1 ~40–60%, LOD2 ~10–25% of LOD0 candidate, separate from Low/Mid/High quality tiers. Never remove floor/anchor/clue silhouette/collision for visual LOD. Low independently authored and approved before Mid/High; no loading hidden High or regenerating terrain.
