## 3. Endings and world continuity
**A — sacrifice:** The younger perspective resolves into the eighteen-year-old existence in the surviving restored reality. Ordinary sky and green grass show what the hero fought to preserve, while weathered markers and records reveal that his former preserved acquaintances now belong to the ancient past. Their lives were real; no blanket amnesia, living duplicates or invented memory of an optional encounter. Claude must distinguish current living residents from historical actors, map the shared geography and safe paths and reserve future burial/spirit recovery/permanent migration as expansion-only. Restored-world exact exploration extent, former third-reality relationship and individual original-family fates require decisions.
**B — refusal:** The preserved landscape carries the original damage and losses; the prior death timeline is replaced in the outcome while experienced history remains meaningful. One age-18 protagonist continues; use immediate recovery as proposed playable start, with a roughly twenty-year reconstruction coda explicitly separate. Claude maps damaged bridges/walls and viable repaired bypasses at actual corresponding locations, then separate later-generation construction/actor layers. Do not assume everyone survives or automatically age the playable character to 38. Individual survivors, final checkpoint and any playable coda need approval.
Both preserve earned gear and level under R76/R84, permit genuinely still-valid optional objectives, and permit optional unlimited NG+. A single active branch per cycle; previous branch remains historical. No forced ending change, automatic reward or cross-world travel introduced to fix a blocked quest. Exact original scene local dates relative to M05 death are the highest writer dependency: only known partial orders are approved for build; block contradictory live scene implementation rather than creating a ghost, resurrection or rewind.

## 5. Claude work packet — repeat for each region/quest, not one oversized generation
Create a bounded packet per area and then per cave/dungeon or encounter using the relevant source ID. This is a suggested workflow, not fixed maps or authorization to create assets. Keep a completed packet registry, stable IDs and dependency order so later tasks consume earlier surveyed results instead of regenerating them.
1. **Narrative brief:** What actually happens, why/how it happens, what the player sees/hears/learns, how the quest/map/cinematic team expresses it. Expand consequential beats with three or four explanatory sentences; preserve approved dialogue and identify draft alternatives, writer-only truth, intentional mystery, missing answer, and testable objective. Read the full script first.
2. **Survey and blockout:** Existing map/asset IDs, region adjacency and approach, local transforms, slopes/elevations and walkable scale, landmark silhouettes and two reality variants. Map proposed dimensions to actual avatar/camera, never assume exact source coordinates. Identify entrances, exterior/interior threshold, floors, stair/ramps, junctions, safe return and optional bypass. Report actual diagram/anchor table.
3. **Architecture and materials:** Low-poly modular stone/timber/cloth/metal/ground kits, material age/wear, repairs, damaged edges and supports, floor/ceiling profile, prop placement, local lighting/weather/audio. Explain how visual difference conveys distinct histories without copying identical buildings; proposed style details not approved canon. Reuse meshes, texture atlases and existing assets where compatible; no revive deleted geometry by default.
4. **Dungeon and traversal:** Room/connection plan and vertical paths, optional chambers, enemies/traps only if authorized by source, telegraph/trigger/reset/bypass, safe checkpoints, independent egress, camera clearance, navmesh/AI and interaction clearance. Voluntary focus with adjustable tolerance, captions, stable non-color cues, walking alternative where authored; collision ready before reveal or optional aerial landing. Release outside hides seam; occupied collision stays intact. No mandatory glide, damage or camera snap absent explicit design.
5. **Actors, quests and cinematics:** Phase-bound actor IDs and availability, NPC positions and safe schedules, objective IDs/receipts, consent, branch conditions, dialogue variants and exactly one presentation per approved beat, triggers and safe skip behavior. Prevent duplicate actor spawning; use localized vista proxies for echoes, not two active full worlds. No arbitrary new deadlines or history resets.
6. **Implementation and evidence:** Return actual branch/commit, files touched, source revision, measured route/collision/navmesh/streaming and UI tests, screenshots if available, WebGPU/fallback and mobile/desktop metrics with hardware/settings, open conflicts, build versus verified status. Work on bounded feature branches with existing controls/security. Do not merge, deploy, spend credits, publish or introduce privileged endpoints as part of this documentation.

| G6 integration and release | QA: all content, mobile/desktop, WebGPU/fallback, performance, security and migration scan | Test results and open risks reviewed; separate explicit integration/publish authorization |

Security requirements: root guest-only policy supersedes legacy login examples. Keep public game entry and character selection; never add auth routes or public service-role access. Validate privileged economy/quest/XP writes against actual supported ownership and encounter context; do not trust client flags. Preserve Base44-managed middleware/error capture and generated router file rules. No claim of passing a security scan in this documentation.
Performance: active timeline/cells only, stable collision before reveal, bounded overlap/view proxies, reuse low-poly materials/meshes, LOD/instancing and occlusion; test actor counts, loading and memory, frame time and stutter, target hardware and low graphics. Do not promise FPS or silently disable valid gear ownership. Avoid loading full old world for coda or NG+ history; archive metadata separate from live scene.
