## Chapter-by-chapter continuity pass
- M01 — The Journey Home: ordinary forest journey establishes a home worth saving. Merchant warning, animal silence, fractured moon, clearing threshold; M02 receives the fragment investigation. Preserve ordinary life before spectacle. Do not foreshadow the older hero through a UI label.
- M02 — The Broken Seal: goddess, fragment, independent wolf shadow and protective arc. M03 owns the transformation. Keep protective warning distinct from a combat objective; preserve the arc silhouette for M05 without asserting identical architecture.
- M03 — The Fractured Beast: corrupted vessel, protective fight, loss of awareness. M04 starts the older playable experience, not a recap. Ensure no victory line says the cosmic threat is finished.
- M04 — The Forgotten War: older hero, civilian route and weapon roles. M05 owns the barrier crossing and physical death. Give retreat success emotional specificity through one recurring civilian group, not an inflated global survival claim.
- M05 — The Silver Path: canonical death at the threshold, life force preserved through silver strand. M06 begins on the younger hand. This is the highest-risk continuity anchor: no ordinary resurrection, replacement body or rewind-as-revival. Subsequent older scenes are real experiences in their own chronology, not necessarily later than the death in local time.
- M06 — A Life Forgotten: silver mark, Goddess conversation, Lyra, Wolfveil and open world. Let the hero doubt the dream explanation rather than accept a cosmological lecture. M07 needs a mandatory geographic lead even if no optional Oath Tree visit occurred.
- M07 — The Mark of a Forgotten Age: matched asymmetric carving and tool-slip scratch, fresh versus ancient evidence. M08 follows the archive lead. Distinguish the personal carving from adjacent insignia; identical shape is evidence, not proof of an identical history.
- M08 — The Missing History: damaged manuscript, intact counterpart, evacuation notice, sanctuary directions. M09 owns the confrontation setup. Keep redaction/copying as evidence, not confirmed culprit attribution; S13 enriches but cannot gate.
- M09 — Beneath the Silver Veil: unnamed goddess, disrupted passage, two skies; ends before physical interception. M10 begins with the older hero acting. Distinct camera/actor ownership prevents replay from exposing identities early.
- M10 — Against the Divine: older intercepts, younger sees resemblance, older does not recognize himself. Escape via experience and intervention, never Celestial Sight. Maintain escape rather than divine defeat; M11 shifts from spectacle to human consequences.
- M11 — Those Left Behind: mining refuge, ventilation, medicine, family and lullaby. M12 owns the counterpart family visit. Keep R31a primary family names Mira/Renn/Tovin and melody asset; no copied identities or forced optional medicine quest.
- M12 — A Song I Know: distinct family names/faces, inherited emblem, record comparison and actual original refuge revisit. The hero may suspect inherited memory without declaring reincarnation. M13 follows the sanctuary lead.
- M13 — The Sanctuary That Remembers: ruined monument versus ongoing refuge work, shared pillar silhouette, recent scratches and names. Give grief room after practical rescue; D19/S23 must not write the same reward or replace required evidence.
- M14 — Three Arrows: assassination aftermath and competing reports from three camps; original civilian retreat. M15 owns escalation. Keep political actors capable of independent choice; no confirmed assassination culprit merely from conflicting testimony.
- M15 — A War That Should Not Be: human retaliation and external invasion share geography and consequences, not attacker identity. Player can prevent a local staged incident without ending the greater war. M16 must acknowledge both prevention and failure variants without assigning the hero blame for all conflict.
- M16 — When the Forgotten Speak: witnesses remember catastrophe, hearing protects people without adjudicating identity. Distinguish memories returning to other people from the hero's actual migrations. M17 moves from testimony to comparative observation.
- M17 — Two Skies, One Wound: observatory records and deliberate convergence strategy. Moon Goddess confirms both lived realities and manipulation of their histories; precise motives and culprit identities remain unresolved. Focus alignment is not a voluntary migration ability.
- M18 — The Twelve and the Price: counsel, protective burden and survivor testimony. Do not require worship or unanimous divine approval. M19 owns the decisive confrontation and outcome commitment. The existing proposal must explicitly distinguish original-body death from later access to older life.
- M19 — The Last Silver Thread: halt forced convergence before deliberation; no forced sacrifice, no global victory inferred. Separation and rebirth are candidate contracts only. Must clarify original-body chronology, shared life force, survivor continuity and younger-life fate before implementation.
- M20 — Where Pain Becomes Peace: branch-specific epilogues, ordinary acts of rebuilding, third reality without invasion history and emotional echoes. Do not make the older epilogue imply resurrection or treat third-world villagers as replacement copies. Preserve healer/guardian status among the Twelve; exact names and other endings remain open.

## Priority continuity correction — chronology ledger
Use four independent fields in all story and terrain briefs: presentation chapter; reality ID; local event ordering (known before/after or unknown); protagonist playable manifestation. M05 is a fixed physical death in the original chronology. A later presentation chapter is not evidence of a later local date. Never label an older scene post-death solely because it appears after M05. M07 fresh carving versus ancient carving is the established exemplar. Keep exact ordering of older scenes relative to M05 explicitly unresolved until author approval; a surviving original population does not require the original body to survive. Separate player knowledge from writer-only truth. Do not use a speculative temporal mechanism as a solved canon fact.

## Proposed bridge dialogue and staging (additive, subject to writer review)
### M10 to M11 — a rescue with no explanation
At the sanctuary's narrowing threshold, the older hero stops long enough to watch the child reach a stable ledge. The camera stays behind his shoulder; the child's face is partially occluded by a fractured pillar, preserving the asymmetry of recognition. The older hero lowers his hand only after the child has disappeared beyond the doorway. No new ability is triggered. On the original side, the sound of a distant warning bell gives way to the refuge's ventilation rattle. Control returns at the existing safe checkpoint, not in a loading tunnel.
Older hero (short breath, looking toward the closed seam): “Did he make it?”
Guard or existing refuge lookout (not claiming to have seen the overlap): “I don't know who you're looking for. The passage this way is open.”
Older hero (after a beat): “Then show me.”
If M10 was skipped cinematically, preserve the rescue and recognition flags; the lookout line still makes sense. No additional NPC identity, reward, mandatory encounter or new map cell.

### M16 to M17 — memories deserve a hearing
At the end of the hearing, a resident takes their testimony back from the clerk and folds it with care. The hero notices how the resident's hands stop trembling once the guard steps away. The keeper points toward the observatory as a place where descriptions can be compared with material evidence, rather than asking the resident to prove who they once were.
Resident (quietly): “If I remember something that never happened here, does that make my life here a lie?”
Hero: “No. You're standing here. That's enough for me to listen.”
Keeper: “Then let us see whether the sky agrees with what they saw.”
If the player refused public disclosure, substitute a private referral and do not expose testimony. M17 minimum evidence remains available; no S-quest required. No declaration of reincarnation or named antagonist.

### M18 to M19 — the question that must be answered
At the existing sanctuary gallery, the hero watches a silver strand bend around the representation of the barrier. His fingers close, remembering a grasp that never completed. The goddess does not explain away the death or promise an extra body. The keeper leaves a blank space in the account instead of guessing.
Hero: “I remember falling. I remember trying to reach you. And I remember going back to those people.”
Moon Goddess (measured, looking at the strand): “The life you lived there was real. The life you live here is real. I will not call either of them a mistake.”
Hero: “Then before I choose anything, I need to know what it means for both of us.”
This line sets the requirement for M19 consequence clarity; it does not solve the underlying chronology. If the approved ending contract is missing, do not commit a canonical outcome. Preview remains noncanonical.
