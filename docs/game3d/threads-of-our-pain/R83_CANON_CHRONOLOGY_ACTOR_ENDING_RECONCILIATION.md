A: awaken at eighteen in the restored surviving timeline, ordinary sky and green ground. The preserved acquaintances are now ancient history/deceased; record their lives truthfully without fabricated memories for skipped quests. Use aged markers and authorized records, not a living duplicate village. Former-world burials/spirit recovery/permanent migration remain expansion directions; do not implement a base-game portal. B: fuse preserved geography with original scars and consequences; separate temporary placement dissipates. Original death frame is replaced in the resulting timeline, not deleted from experienced journal history. Continued gameplay at age eighteen is the intended baseline; use R65 immediate safe recovery as proposed start, with coda explicitly separate and later. Exact checkpoint/coda playability needs final approval. Never infer survival or family descendants from a rebuilding scene.
Both: earned equipment and level continuity under R76; no forced D/S completion. Persistent quests may continue only with an approved live actor/route and valid event state. A historical record can satisfy a record objective only when authored. Confirmed closed event → missed this cycle, not automatic reward. NG+ is a new campaign cycle and is not an in-world rewind or portal. It can reproduce prior authored events without reversing the previous ending record.
