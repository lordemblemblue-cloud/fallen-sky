## Scene 1 — A ring in the rain
Age fifteen. Rain runs down the roots of a tree that has grown around the crypt entrance. Damp ash drifts from an abandoned cooking fire behind the rock spur. Someone has scraped moss from a burial niche, leaving a rectangle of pale stone. An old bandit sketch has been scratched into a wooden tally board on a nearby ledge. The map loaned during S01 can provide the same clue, but the board is available even if the merchant was never encountered. The hero traces the circular glyph with a finger and looks toward the distant pillar.
Hero (curious, stepping backward): “They drew the same thing twice. Or I'm looking at it wrong.”
If S02 is active, he can add, “This isn't the chamber they were talking about.” If finished, “Another room? They never mentioned this one.” The player moves until niche, glyph and pillar complete a ring; no camera movement is forced. A subtle chord and high-contrast outline mark alignment. The first perspective persists even if the older life is not currently available.
## Scene 2 — The shelter promise
Age eighteen. Beneath the corresponding natural arch, a guard writes shelter numbers on small metal tokens. A family has lost contact with an uncle. The guard listens, gives them a token and returns to the registry. The older protagonist already wears the Gravekeeper Charm.
Guard (measured, hands deliberately steady): “Keep this where someone can find it.”
Civilian: “What if he comes here after we leave?”
Guard: “Then we'll know where to start looking. We won't erase his name.”
Hero: “You're keeping a list of everyone?”
Guard (glances down the road): “Everyone we can.”
The player can help place a spare token on the registry stand or observe it in place. Its rim, stone edge and arch resolve the corresponding ring. The hero suddenly smells damp roots in the dry shelter. He checks his boots and returns to helping. No explanation of the two lives is offered.
## Scene 3 — The annex that wasn't on the map
With both first-perspective flags recorded, holding focus reveals seams in a burial wall behind the camp. A dry landing inside provides a checkpoint. The left gallery holds empty niches and a damaged memorial; the right descends toward a ceremonial chamber. A few loose stones fall harmlessly ahead of the player, warning of a weak patch that can be bypassed through the gallery. Neither S02's grave offering nor S03 is required. The unidentified witness here is not confirmed as the S03 guardian; if S03 has concluded, do not reset or replay its encounter.
A figure sits beside an empty platform. The robe almost dissolves into the damp wall. It looks beyond the hero rather than at his weapon.
Witness (exhausted): “Did you see the silver light?”
Hero: “Where?”
Witness: “Above the trees. We thought someone had come for us.” [Long pause; looks at the platform.] “The light left us waiting.”
Choice — Who were you waiting for? Witness: “Anyone who still remembered our names.” Choice — Did it hurt? Witness: “I remember warmth. Then silence.” Choice — Listen. The witness smooths an empty space on the platform as though straightening a blanket. No option establishes who caused the light or the fate of the missing.
## Scene 4 — The charm and the second vista
A dark metal charm lies in a shallow recess beyond the platform. The circular engraving is worn along one edge, as though somebody held it for years. The younger hero closes his hand around it. In the original shelter the older hero touches the charm already attached to his equipment. Its weight feels newly received. He says nothing and checks the waiting family's token. The younger hero hears a pen scratching paper even though the chamber is silent.
At an upper slit, the player aligns a niche-shaped rock, arch and ridge. The corresponding shelter becomes visible; the guard says, “We'll keep looking. That's what I promised.” The older hero hears rain on roots. The younger lowers his hand: “I hope somebody came back.” A dry maintenance passage returns to the exterior. Reward is the catalog Gravekeeper Charm, balance or cosmetic fallback TBD; never grant another charm to the older self.
