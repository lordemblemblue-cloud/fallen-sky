## A. P01 scene sequence and design envelope
One physical forest and one shared clearing. Age 15 throughout M01–M03; no original-world body/scene or dimensional explanation before M04. Wolfveil town itself is M06, not a required opening stop. Primary route 100–150 m suggested, ascent 6–12 m, path 3–5 m; chainage from R87A is walked distance along route, never world coordinates. Merchant turnout 6x8 m, canopy gap at least roughly 12 m if actual sightline supports. Clearing 24–34 m across with 1–2 m shallow depression, 5–8 m safe shelf. M03 arena 30–40 m candidate only if required after reach/camera tests: reconcile with shared M02 footprint before building; do not swap to duplicate scene. Ring 3–5 m clear, protected central zone 5–7 m. Actual controller clearance, slopes, turn radii, sightlines and bounds TBD. Preserve geology for later original-world counterparts without building those now.
Route graph (text schematic; no surveyed distances):
SPAWN -> ROOT_BEND -> RIVER_BEND / BOULDER -> MERCHANT -> SKY_WINDOW -> FORK
ROOT_BEND <-> optional stream loop <-> RIVER_BEND
FORK -> homeward loop -> FORK
FORK -> UPPER_VISTA or STREAM_PATH -> CLEARING_EDGE -> M02_APPROACH
APPROACH -> PERIMETER_N -> FRAGMENT_VIEW -> PROTECT_SAFE
APPROACH -> PERIMETER_S -> WOLF_VIEW -> PROTECT_SAFE
both perimeter routes -> RETREAT -> trail; PROTECT_SAFE -> M03_START only after readiness
M03_START -> connected RING_N/E/S/W -> LURE_A/B -> ROOT_FINISH -> AFTER -> TRANSITION (M04-ready)
RING -> RETREAT -> M02 perimeter only before fight or confirmed reset; no enemy pursuing into forest. Lure points not mandatory detour if normal role damage interrupts. Do not force optional jump, swim, flight, focus or relic.

Do not equate M03 ordinary defeat with M05 canonical death. Actual defeat plus aftermath; no false success dialogue, dead target release and no duplicate reward. Keep Entity exact “You cannot keep them from us”; Goddess warning and one optional aftermath response, no early cosmology.
