# S04 — Sworn at the Gate
R56 | Source: R55 cumulative master, original S04 catalog and M06/M14 expansions. Optional oath at Wolfveil Oath Tree, Lyra and Gate Warden; age-18 refugee registry. New dialogue, prop details, state rules and geometry are proposals. No required faction allegiance.
## Scene 1 — A knot in the tree
Age fifteen, after M06's introduction to Wolfveil. A market cart creaks past the fountain. The Oath Tree casts a broken pattern of light over its stone surround; ribbons hang at different heights, some faded almost white. Lyra is untying a ribbon that has wound itself around a growing branch. She notices the hero watching and offers him a loose end instead of immediately recruiting him.
Lyra (matter-of-fact, working the knot): “Hold that, would you? It'll cut into the bark if we leave it.”
Hero: “What are all these for?”
Lyra: “People who promised to look after the road. Some live here. Some were just passing through.”
Hero: “And if they leave?”
Lyra (frees the branch, hands him the ribbon): “An oath means what you do after saying it. The tree doesn't follow you.”
The player may tie a neutral ribbon, promise help without a ribbon, or decline. Lyra accepts each without disappointment animation or a hidden morality penalty. For a refusal: “All right. You're still welcome at the fountain.” For a promise: “Then remember the people, not the knot.” The interaction never changes faction, equipment or combat permissions. The camera remains behind the player; a reachable interaction at waist height is an alternative to the hanging ribbons.
## Scene 2 — The count at the gate
A gate bell calls attention toward the southern gate fields. A warden walks up the market lane carrying a slate with three empty arrival lines. He stops short of the tree to avoid interrupting an oath. If the hero did not speak to Lyra, the same warden is encountered naturally on the gate approach.
Warden (glances toward the road): “Travelers are overdue. Three names on the list. The last wagon should have been here before the market opened.”
Hero: “I'll look.”
Warden: “Don't go chasing every sound in the woods. Start where the road bends. If somebody's hurt, send them back here.”
Player can ask for a description, volunteer immediately, or leave. Description: Tome carries a tied bundle, Henut has a wrapped jar, Livilla travels with a small keepsake. These are identification cues, not mandatory inventory objectives. The warden points down the road rather than painting all three rescue markers on the minimap. The gate remains open for ordinary travel.
## Scene 3 — A promise on paper
Age eighteen, in the original reality during an available evacuation interval. A registry clerk at a sheltered road junction ties string around a bundle of refugee slips. A family waits for a missing relative. The older protagonist can voluntarily help carry the registry bundle to a protected desk or simply observe the clerk's routine.
Clerk (trying to keep the pages dry): “I can keep a name here. I can't promise they'll arrive.”
Hero: “Then keep the name.”
Clerk: “Even after we move?”
Hero: “Especially then.”
He feels a strand of rough cloth across his fingers though he holds paper. In the younger life, the wind briefly sounds like pages turning. Neither understands the resemblance. The older experience is real, not a memory simulation; it is not a compulsory new migration or proof that these are the same people. If its authored interval is unavailable, the younger quest remains completable.
## Scene 4 — A road worth taking
At the younger gate bend, wagon ruts and a torn strip of cloth lead toward the fields. The hero can report the trail to the warden or proceed to investigate; either establishes the lead to S05. If S05 was already completed independently, the warden instead acknowledges actual arrival records. No second rescue scene is spawned. If the hero refused the oath, the warden says, “You didn't have to promise anything to tell me that.” Lyra's later greeting uses the recorded choice, never an assumed pledge.
## Quest contract and consequences
S04_AVAILABLE after M06 public introduction → TREE_CHOICE (optional) / WARDEN_SEEN → TRAIL_FOUND or S05_RECEIPT_OBSERVED → REPORT or PROCEED → COMPLETE. Declining a ribbon does not decline aid; leaving the region pauses rather than fails. S05 may start independently; S04 completion is not an M07, M14 or faction gate. Save choice, clue and completion receipt separately; retry at public gate, no duplicate rewards. Proposed modest XP and optional neutral ribbon cosmetic; no stat advantage for swearing. Older registry assistance has its own ambient flag, not a second S04 reward. Younger believes the promise is an ordinary civic custom; older believes preserving names is practical duty. Developer truth is a shared emotional echo, not identical history. No forced combat, paired focus or relic.
