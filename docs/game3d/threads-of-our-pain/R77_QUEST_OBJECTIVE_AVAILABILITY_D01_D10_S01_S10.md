## 1. Classification contract
P = persistent within its authored valid world and phase, not proof of access in either ending; C = conditionally revisitable only through an existing authored access/record path; E = event-exclusive genuine action/observation, missed for the current cycle once its opportunity is actually confirmed closed; U = unresolved exact gate or post-ending accessibility. E is a design class, not an assertion that a specific closing chapter is known. Classify objective lanes, not just whole quests. A local objective can remain available when a historical optional step expires. No fabricated receipts, arbitrary rewinds, new portals, resurrection or automatic reward grants. An authored retrospective may complete a quest only where its source permits; it cannot convert missed participation into a rescue or observation receipt.

## 4. Journal and world behavior
Display AVAILABLE, WAITING_FOR_VALID_CONDITIONS, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, or MISSED_THIS_CYCLE at objective level. Only use MISSED when an authored irreversible closing condition is true. Do not warn about an invented chapter deadline. Optional historical lore expiration does not falsely fail a complete local quest. Example: after M14, S06 offers the actual retrospective; its live-parley record is missed if not observed, and a record of NPC independent arrival is not player rescue credit. Example: an owned D08 lantern remains usable even if its recollection is unfinished; S10 access still requires the actual reveal. An active danger interval only defers a focus, not permanently fails it. In sacrifice, do not stage a live deceased actor in a historical ruin; in refusal, validate individual survival and actual routes before offering an old objective. Both ending branches retain equipment and progression. New Game Plus creates new cycle opportunity flags and retains prior receipts/history and unique item ownership per R76.
