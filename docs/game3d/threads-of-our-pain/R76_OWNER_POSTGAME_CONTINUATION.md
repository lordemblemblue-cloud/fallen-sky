# R76 — Owner-approved continuation after the ending
27 November 2026 | Documentation contract, not runtime implementation.

> **R76A — owner clarification:** [Missable chapter quests and New Game Plus](R76A_MISSABLE_QUESTS_AND_NEW_GAME_PLUS.md). Persistent quests remain available; genuinely chapter-exclusive opportunities can close for the current cycle. NG+ at increased difficulty is the preferred recovery design; no magical rewind or automatic rewards. Exact classification and scaling pending.

## Owner decisions
The owner confirmed that players can continue the quests they have not done after the campaign and keep all their equipment because they need it to progress further. Apply this to both ending routes discussed immediately before the confirmation. These requirements supersede any R75/R75A proposal that would prevent postgame quest completion or strip earned equipment solely because an ending was chosen. All other R75/R75A proposals remain subject to approval. Preserve R64A/R65 story consequences and R66 quest-specific truth.

## 1. Equipment is carried forward, not reset
Retain all legitimately owned equipment, including equipped and stored gear, relics, upgrades, enhancement and socket data. Never delete or downgrade equipment merely because a player completes M20 or chooses a particular ending. Distinct items remain distinct; a paired relic entitlement is granted only once, and sold, consumed or dismantled gear is not recreated. Quest-bound borrowed props and temporary effects are not permanent earned equipment. If destination compatibility is a genuine problem, retain ownership and provide a safe way to recover and use gear; do not turn this into a progression blocker. The intent is usable equipment for subsequent quests, stronger encounters and future progression, not just a cosmetic archive. Retaining levels, skill ranks, currency, materials and other earned state is recommended from R75 but not separately confirmed in this turn.

## 2. The campaign continues
M20 marks a story milestone, not the end of play. Unfinished and unstarted optional adventures and paired discoveries remain completable after either ending where their authored conditions still hold. Chapter-exclusive objectives may close for the current cycle under R76A. Preserve actual objective progress, item-claim receipts, evidence, consent and second-vista flags; never auto-complete them or reset the player's journal. Completed quests stay completed. Legitimate outstanding quest rewards must be able to advance the player's continuing character; a non-credit cinematic gallery or an isolated replay alone does not satisfy the owner's requirement. No requirement to complete all 105 optional IDs before M20.

Some original-world encounters cannot occur in the active post-ending geography without contradicting established actor lifetimes. A guardian at peace cannot be made hostile again; a deceased person cannot reappear as a present-day living quest giver. The earlier proposed historical revisit context is superseded where it would reopen a genuinely missed chapter. Use only existing, story-authorized revisits in the active cycle; New Game Plus is the preferred design for experiencing expired opportunities in a fresh cycle. Future alternate item acquisition requires separate approval. Keep separate optional alternate-ending replay isolated unless a later policy explicitly changes that rule. No fabricated historical completion, duplicate reward or forced ending reversal.

## 3. Claude's terrain and UI handoff
Use R65 ending anchor routes and R67/R68 actor phases. Maintain a safe playable destination, a readable quest journal and genuine return routes. Classify each D01–D50 and S01–S55 objective as persistent, chapter-exclusive, conditionally revisitable or unresolved; map its legitimate context, route, interaction, receipt, reward and safe exit. In the restored reality, use historical landmarks without presenting deceased acquaintances as current residents. In fused recovery, honor actual damage, survivors and completed repairs. Do not construct new portals or overwrite history to solve a schedule gap. Report missing anchors and unresolved encounter windows rather than invent them. Historical context loading should unload the prior scene as appropriate, preserving the selected ending and earned equipment; verify performance on target devices.
