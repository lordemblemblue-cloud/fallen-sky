# R76 — Owner-approved continuation after the ending
27 November 2026 | Documentation contract, not runtime implementation.

## Owner decisions
The owner confirmed that players can continue the quests they have not done after the campaign and keep all their equipment because they need it to progress further. Apply this to both ending routes discussed immediately before the confirmation. These requirements supersede any R75/R75A proposal that would prevent postgame quest completion or strip earned equipment solely because an ending was chosen. All other R75/R75A proposals remain subject to approval. Preserve R64A/R65 story consequences and R66 quest-specific truth.

## 1. Equipment is carried forward, not reset
Retain all legitimately owned equipment, including equipped and stored gear, relics, upgrades, enhancement and socket data. Never delete or downgrade equipment merely because a player completes M20 or chooses a particular ending. Distinct items remain distinct; a paired relic entitlement is granted only once, and sold, consumed or dismantled gear is not recreated. Quest-bound borrowed props and temporary effects are not permanent earned equipment. If destination compatibility is a genuine problem, retain ownership and provide a safe way to recover and use gear; do not turn this into a progression blocker. The intent is usable equipment for subsequent quests, stronger encounters and future progression, not just a cosmetic archive. Retaining levels, skill ranks, currency, materials and other earned state is recommended from R75 but not separately confirmed in this turn.

## 2. The campaign continues
M20 marks a story milestone, not the end of play. Unfinished and unstarted optional adventures and paired discoveries must remain completable after either ending. Preserve actual objective progress, item-claim receipts, evidence, consent and second-vista flags; never auto-complete them or reset the player's journal. Completed quests stay completed. Legitimate outstanding quest rewards must be able to advance the player's continuing character; a non-credit cinematic gallery or an isolated replay alone does not satisfy the owner's requirement. No requirement to complete all 105 optional IDs before M20.

Some original-world encounters cannot occur in the active post-ending geography without contradicting established actor lifetimes. A guardian at peace cannot be made hostile again; a deceased person cannot reappear as a present-day living quest giver. Proposed design: use a clearly labeled, phase-valid historical quest context for outstanding objectives, linked to the continuing character's quest ledger through validated, once-only receipts. This is a game-system proposal, not a confirmed time-travel power or new portal, and its exact access method, checkpoint scope and reward authorization require approval and testing. Keep separate optional alternate-ending replay isolated unless a later policy explicitly changes that rule. No fabricated historical completion, duplicate reward or forced ending reversal.

## 3. Claude's terrain and UI handoff
Use R65 ending anchor routes and R67/R68 actor phases. Maintain a safe playable destination, a readable quest journal and genuine return routes. Map each outstanding D01–D50 and S01–S55 objective to an actual phase-valid context, route, interaction, required receipt, reward destination and safe exit. In the restored reality, use historical landmarks without presenting deceased acquaintances as current residents. In fused recovery, honor actual damage, survivors and completed repairs. Do not construct new portals or overwrite history to solve a schedule gap. Report missing anchors and unresolved encounter windows rather than invent them. Historical context loading should unload the prior scene as appropriate, preserving the selected ending and earned equipment; verify performance on target devices.

## 4. Acceptance matrix — proposed tests, not executed
| Scenario | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Either ending with equipped and stored upgraded items | All legitimately owned equipment retained with enhancement and socket state; usable according to established requirements |
| Both endings with zero optional content | Remaining discoveries and adventures are completable by a valid postgame route; no forced completion |
| Partially completed discovery, relic already claimed | Keep claim and objective state; allow remaining valid alignment, no duplicate equipment |
| Partial adventure with a phase-bound actor | Preserve genuine progress; valid historical interaction required, no present-day actor resurrection |
| Earn outstanding reward after ending | Validated once-only reward advances continuing character, including gear upgrades where applicable |
| Repeat claim, repeated confirmation, interrupted transition | No duplicate rewards or loss of owned gear, quest or ending state |
| Sold/consumed equipment | No resurrection through old quest receipt |
| Destination/archive failure | Preserve recoverable progression and show retry or recovery, no destructive reset |
| Separate alternate-ending replay | Does not silently overwrite committed ending or duplicate economy; exact replay policy pending |

Respect existing guest-entry, permissions and authoritative reward validation; no client-supplied privileged grants.

## 5. Remaining decisions
Exact access mechanism for earlier encounters, phase windows, level/XP/currency and other progression categories, pre-finale archive and alternate-ending replay policy, restored-world scope, playable coda timing, S53/S55 specifics and expansion-only spirit migration remain open where not previously approved. R76 locks only the two owner decisions above. Source coverage remains 125/125 narrative entries and 105/105 detailed source scheduling reviews, not proof that any gameplay route works. No code, terrain, merge or deployment in this revision.
