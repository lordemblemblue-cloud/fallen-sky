## 2. Four orders, three distinct relationships
Track (1) local O sequence, (2) local P sequence, (3) experienced sequence of the protagonist, (4) presentation/checkpoint order. Do not infer one from another. Also distinguish local causal influence, a correspondence between sites/events, and a progression/availability gate. The first may change physical state locally; the second may permit bounded overlap if authored; the third controls player access and is not a cosmological law. A shared landmark is not automatically an overlap or a portal.

## 3. The hard causal question and three models
If M05 creates the link for the first time as a causal event, that link causes M06, which permits M10, where the older intervenes before his own local M05 under candidate A. Treating all presentation, local and overlap edges as a single forward clock creates a loop. A simple topological sort of only the two local graphs cannot establish metaphysical consistency. Do not call A solved because those separate graphs are acyclic.
A1 (recommended discussion candidate): underlying correspondence is not created by the death. M05 is the point at which the Goddess preserves a life force that remains connected; it is not necessarily the origin of the connection. The correspondence relates actual events without a universal before/after, and a fixed campaign history includes the real M10 encounter. This can permit nonlinear awareness without a new time-travel tool. Requires owner approval of an underlying connection whose reach includes pre-death O events, and consistency of cross-world causes. It does not establish why those two lives share existence or prove all effects are predetermined. The line “remained connected” in M18 supports this reading but does not prove it.
A2: M05 creates a connection with backward causal reach. This requires an explicitly authorized self-consistent causal loop and a rule for which information may cross. More difficult for optional decisions, risks bootstrap objects/information and arbitrary rewriting. Do not implement or imply as canon.
A3: leave origin intentionally unknown while approving only bounded lived correspondence at already-authored encounters. This permits staging and much writing but is not sufficient to ship a live chronology-dependent migration system. Recommend A1 as writer-side hypothesis and A3 as player-facing presentation until M18; even M18 only discloses what is known. If owner rejects A, return to dependency register before changing M05 or M10.
Proposed consistency boundary: an already experienced, fixed authored fact cannot be silently contradicted by a later-presented live choice. This is a constraint on writing and save state, not a claim all history is preordained. If an observation is unconditional, the later action must be optional-compatible or already entailed; if an outcome depends on optional action, show only the actual recorded outcome or a neutral baseline that asserts no player action. Do not auto-complete the quest to resolve a contradiction. If no compatible actual scene exists, defer the scene and flag writer discrepancy; do not manufacture actor or receipt. No general revisit to rewrite history. Branch A/B is the sole approved finale transformation; NG+ is a new campaign cycle, not this ability.
