## D26 — Scene 1: Three hands, one wall

Age fifteen. A neutral road passes beneath a limestone escarpment where the old meeting grounds of three cultures sit in a sheltered hollow. The exterior has no grand palace; it is a small stone terrace used by diplomats and road workers. Rain has softened the soil around three carved panels. One uses a repeating angular border, another a broad curving line, the third a sequence of small rectangular cuts. Their workmanship differs, but the same limestone and tool-wear suggest that someone assembled the panels together rather than randomly collecting them. The player can see the panels before meeting any quest giver. A diplomat sits on the terrace, untying a wet bundle of papers.

Diplomat (annoyed at himself): “I left the satchel under the bench.”

Guard: “It slid when the rain came through. There's a grate below the terrace.”

Hero: “I'll look if you want.”

The satchel quest is S37 and can be declined. The diplomat's assistant retrieves it later if declined; the terrace remains explorable. No clue is removed by skipping the quest. A fragment of carved stone visible from the ordinary cave entrance catches the hero's attention. A thin line from one culture's border continues onto another panel. It is not proof of a specific ancient alliance, but it suggests the designs were meant to fit.

The hero can examine the three carvings from an accessible stone shelf. In focus mode the player adjusts their own camera until the separate outlines form a shared crest: a broad base, two asymmetric side strokes and a joining central line. Use the actual M08 crest only if the owner confirms that link; default distinct coalition crest to avoid prematurely proving sanctuary connection. There is no required camera zoom and no exact pixel alignment. A subtle contour cue, caption and optional widened tolerance let the player recognize the composition. Age fifteen alignment persists after leaving.

Hero: “Those edges meet.”

Guard (walking over if available): “They do from here. Never noticed that.”

## D26 — Scene 3: Beneath the meeting ground

The lower corridor contains an old counterweighted partition. A cracked wooden guide and grit falling from the upper slot warn that it may shift. Light enters through two high limestone vents; dust gathers along the unoccupied seats. A shallow channel once carried water away from the hall. The player can walk around the center freely and inspect the join between panels. The council hall was built in the preserved world's own history; it need not have existed in the original world.

## S37 — Scene 1: The lost satchel

Witness the diplomat searching the bench after rain, or speak with the guard on the neutral road. The satchel contains signed meeting notes, not a secret master plan. The guard knows it fell through a damaged drainage grate. The diplomat fears losing proof that a conversation happened, because rival delegates are already disputing what was said. Player may help immediately, ask for a description, or decline. If declined, a later visit offers help with the mural investigation instead of forcing a lost-object reset.

Diplomat: “I can rewrite what I said. I can't rewrite what they agreed to.”

Hero: “What does it look like?”

Diplomat: “Brown leather. One brass buckle. The other broke.”

Guard: “I'll keep the entrance clear.”

The player enters the ordinary limestone cave through a visible path. The satchel is in a shallow debris pocket, not the hidden D26 council hall. Three choices: follow a dry ramp and retrieve it, clear a small obstruction with a local lever, or ask the guard to assist at the safe landing. The wet side passage is optional; shallow footing, runoff audio and visible rails telegraph slippery terrain. Failed traversal resets the current leg without destroying the papers or requiring a new quest. A recovery path leads back to the dry ramp. No mandatory swim or combat.
