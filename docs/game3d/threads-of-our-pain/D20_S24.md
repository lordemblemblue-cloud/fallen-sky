## D20 Scene 1 — The plaque without a face

The younger protagonist approaches the sanctuary pass in late afternoon. A weathered metal lantern hook hangs from an outcrop above a low stone shelter. Beyond it, two hills overlap, leaving a narrow notch of sky. A keeper is sweeping grit from an old plaque. The inscription commemorates an unnamed watchman and the open pass; no portrait or rank is given. A rusted token has fallen into a shallow drainage groove. The keeper is more concerned about preserving the plaque than identifying a famous hero. The protagonist notices that the hook is bent in a way that seems familiar, and holds his empty hand beneath it as if expecting a lantern handle.

Keeper (still sweeping): “Someone kept the pass open.”
Hero: “Who?”
Keeper: “No name on it. Whoever wrote this wanted us to remember the road.”
Hero (watching the notch): “Was there a light here?”
Keeper: “There used to be one. People could find the bend by it.”

The player can pick up the token, ask about the plaque, or simply observe the hook. Token investigation belongs to S24, while hook and hills remain discoverable without accepting it. If the player asks whether the watchman died, the keeper says, “I don't know. The plaque doesn't say.” Do not imply the protagonist was the commemorated watchman or settle the watchman's fate. The younger hero thinks the place resembles a dream of staying awake too long.

## Scene 2 — Just an hour

Age eighteen, original sanctuary. The same hills outline a moonlit pass. There is no ancient plaque here; a small campfire burns beside the guard's shelter and two hooks hold lanterns at different heights. Behind the guard, refugees move in small groups toward a sheltered descent. He tries to stand when the protagonist approaches, then rests a hand on his knee. His words are slightly delayed, as though he has to remember what he meant to say. He is not cowardly or incompetent; he has been on duty far too long and cannot safely continue without relief.

Watchman (embarrassed): “Just an hour.”
Hero: “Go. I'll call if they come.”
Watchman: “You know the bend? They can't see you from below.”
Hero: “Show me where the light goes.”

He points out the higher hook, fire and lower hook. The older protagonist already carries a night-watch cloak and spare lantern from his equipment issue. He hangs his lantern at the station and checks that the campfire remains visible from the path. As he moves between two stones, the fire and hook contours form a flame silhouette. The player can align it before accepting S24 or after securing the watch station. The watchman may remain at the post if the quest is declined; a short baseline conversation makes all focus geometry available regardless of quest state. No player is required to leave an exhausted guard unattended to obtain a relic.

The slit gives a view of the ordinary peaceful road; the player may notice a traveler following it. ## S24 — The Night Watch's Debt: independent quest script

Age fifteen trigger: witness the keeper tending the plaque, inspect the rusted token in the groove. Keeper: “That's been sitting there since the last storm. It belongs up here, not in the mud.” The player may clean the token at the keeper's bench, leave it for conservation or record its outline without taking it. Keeper: “Don't polish away what you came to find.” A confirmed record sets S24_TOKEN_RECORDED. The token is evidence, not a combat reward or the D20 cloak. If collected before acceptance, allow retrospective acknowledgment. Keeper keeps the physical token after the record unless player is explicitly granted a separate replica (not required). No additional quest ID.

Age eighteen trigger: observe the watchman's fatigue. Accept, arrange a shared watch with a nearby relief guard, or decline and return later. Shared watch is not an automatic failure or lower moral score. Watchman: “If the fire goes out, the lower road loses the bend.” Hero: “Then we'll keep the fire in sight.” The player learns three safe stations: bend lookout, lantern hook and rest shelter. No real-time hour-long wait: the narrative hour is condensed into three short defense intervals with recovery windows. Proposed active intervals 25–40 seconds each, recovery windows 20–30 seconds, subject to playtest. No timer while dialogue, menus or loading. The watchman sleeps in a protected shelter out of enemy navigation, and civilians wait at safe nodes when danger approaches.

Interval 1: a distant creature silhouette appears beyond the bend with an audio/visual warning. Player can engage one small enemy or signal the relief guard and lure it away from civilian path. Watchman is never a required damage sponge. Interval 2: wind threatens the lantern; secure it at the hook or ask the helper to do so. Threat approaches only after the player has finished the interaction and can move. Interval 3: two staggered threats approach, never spawn behind the player in the shelter. Defend the line, draw them toward guard support, or signal a sheltered reroute. The final segment ends once the threat is repelled and the path confirmed safe. Accessibility mode may lengthen telegraphs, reduce simultaneous enemy count, or assign guard assistance without changing dialogue. No actual sleep timer after leaving the area.

Recovery scenes: after interval one, a traveler pauses. “Is he all right?” Hero: “He's resting. Keep going.” After interval two, the helper says, “You can sit while I look down the bend.” The player may accept and recover ordinary combat resources. On defeat, restore the last completed interval at safe start; watchman and civilians remain safe, no story deaths or permanent equipment losses. On abandonment or migration, relief guard holds the post, pending progress persists, and return dialogue says, “We've kept it open so far.” Decline leaves baseline NPC behavior and focus anchors intact. If the player has not recorded the token yet, older watch completion remains saved and report can be finished later. No repeat farming.

Completion: both token record and watch completion, then acknowledgment in each relevant reality or retrospective dialogue. Younger keeper: “It wasn't a name they were missing. It was someone who would take the next turn.” Hero: “Then leave the hook where it is.” Keeper: “I will.” Older guard gets up, stretches his fingers and resumes ordinary patrol. Proposed XP, local sanctuary goodwill and ordinary repair supplies once; no duplicate cloak. Distinct world state: keeper stabilizes hook and plaque, old shelter remains a safe rest site; original guard resumes shift with helper available. S24 independent of D20 and M03; do not require optional night-watch cloak to defend. No assertion of historical identity across realities.
