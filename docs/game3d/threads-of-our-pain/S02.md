# S02 — The Thief's Treasure
R48 — catalog-aligned expansion. West Root Cavern: camp around sealed ancient chamber, unusual grave offering, original-world supply cache and recognizable latch. S03 owns the spirit's defense/restoration and full testimony. No unique reward specified by catalog; proposed modest supplies and standard XP only.
## Scene 1 — Following somebody else's map
Age fifteen. The path west of the roots narrows between stone shoulders. Muddy boot prints end beside snapped bracken. The merchant's loaned map, if acquired, shows a notch corresponding to an old mine; without it, the prints and torch glow provide a natural lead. A bandit is flattening a bent cooking pot against a rock. He notices the hero but keeps the pot in his hand.
Bandit (wary): “Lost, are you?”
Hero: “I'm looking for the place on this map.” [Without map: “What are you digging for?”]
Bandit (looks toward the rear wall): “We only found the outer room.”
Hero: “Then why are you still here?”
Bandit: “Because the man who paid us wanted what was behind it.” [Rubs soot from thumb.] “He didn't tell us how to open it.”
No patron name or guilt is established. The player can inspect the door, ask about stolen property if S01 is active, or leave. Returning merchant property creates S01's existing recovery receipt, not another reward. Hostile camp members engage only after escalation or crossing a warned boundary. Peaceful inspection, combat or a disused side tunnel each reach the chamber.
## Scene 2 — Cold stone, warm latch
The mine's timber supports end where dressed ceremonial stone begins. Torchlight becomes weak against a gray wall and shallow recessed latch. The hero removes his glove to test the stone. His expression shifts from curiosity to caution.
Hero (quiet): “Why is the stone warm?”
Bandit (if friendly): “That's what I said. No fire behind it, either.”
The player examines a pivoting stone catch and counterweight. Two grooves show direction; a worn lip shows the catch can be lifted. The optional mining route passes a collapsed timber support and reaches the same catch from a service alcove, not the inner crypt. Lift then rotate to open a narrow threshold. Wrong rotation returns to neutral with a dull click; never consumes a key or causes unavoidable damage. A safe ledge permits departure and retry.
## Scene 3 — The cache under a different sky
Age eighteen. The corresponding rock shoulder contains an evacuation supply cache, not a buried ceremonial chamber. Stacked sacks smell of damp cloth; a quartermaster struggles to move a crate without blocking the doorway. The hero recognizes the latch movement.
Quartermaster: “Can you open the inner store? The children are waiting for blankets.”
Hero (already reaching): “Lift first. If you turn it, it jams.”
Quartermaster: “You worked here before?”
Hero (pauses): “I don't think so.”
The player can open the cache and move a crate to a staging mark. This is optional original-world exploration, not a requirement for S02. The younger hero feels the pressure of a glove against his bare palm; the older hears stone scraping though his own latch has stopped. Neither knows why. No discovery reward here; D03 owns the ring and charm.
## Scene 4 — The offering
Behind the threshold a low stone table holds an unusual offering wrapped in faded cloth. It is not the Gravekeeper Charm. The hero unfolds just enough to see a deliberately arranged object, then stops as a voice comes from beyond the wall.
Voice (unlocalized, strained): “Put it back.”
Hero (withdraws his hand): “Who's there?”
Bandit (if present, steps back): “I told you there was something in there.”
Choices: retrieve the offering as evidence (wrap carefully, no sale); negotiate for its protection (record it in place, bandit withdraws); or leave and return. Inspection plus committed custody/protection completes S02. Do not trigger S03 combat here. If S03 already restored the offering, show the cloth in place, acknowledge the restoration and complete S02 by investigation rather than undoing it. A faint sound leads toward the inner crypt; player can follow or leave. S03 retains the spirit's thousand-year claim, defensive manifestation and restoration choice.
