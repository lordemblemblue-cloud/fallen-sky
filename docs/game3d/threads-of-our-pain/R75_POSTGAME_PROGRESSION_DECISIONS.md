# R75 — What survives the ending: progression and save decisions
27 November 2026 | Owner decision package, not approved gameplay. R74 completes 105/105 source-level encounter scheduling reviews. Read R64A, R65, R66 and R69–R74 first. R63 is historical where it conflicts. No new quest IDs or exact dates.

## 1. Preserve the player's work without undoing the story
Both outcomes bring the protagonist into his age-eighteen existence. Sacrifice restores a peaceful reality and places the people he knew at fifteen in ancient history. Refusal fuses the preserved geography with the original destruction, replaces the earlier death timeline and supports continued play. The approximately twenty-year reconstruction coda is not automatically a playable time jump. The restored world's exploration scope and its relationship to the earlier proposed third reality remain open. Neither outcome grants permanent spirit migration in the base campaign.

Recommended policy, pending owner confirmation: retain earned character progression on both routes. An ending is a narrative consequence, not an equipment penalty. Preserve the player's attained level, earned skill ranks, legitimate unlocks, cosmetics and achievements. Preserve ordinary acquired equipment and resources through a single validated inventory reconciliation, with reality-bound quest keys and temporary effects handled separately. This does not mean physically transferring every historical object or adding both avatars' inventories together. A relic already owned by the older hero is not granted again when the younger hero finds its counterpart. Keep the dramatic older-touch reaction; deduplicate only the entitlement where the two records describe the same item. Distinct legitimate equipment remains distinct.

If the two lives currently have incompatible inventories or level sources, the engineering team must return the actual schema and a proposed reconciliation map before a shipping transition. Do not invent an existing shared wallet, select an arbitrary maximum for every stat, or sum two skill trees. Retain original values in recoverable source records until approval; no destructive conversion.

## 2. Save and replay recommendation
Create a durable pre-finale checkpoint after M19 combat ends and before the player commits to either outcome. The active campaign continues into its chosen branch. Offer a separate clearly labeled pre-finale adventure copy to finish missed experiences and optionally review the other ending, if the owner approves. That copy is noncanonical relative to the completed campaign: its world state, inventory, currency, quest flags and reward writes stay isolated. It cannot replace the committed ending by accident or move items back to the active save. Account-wide cosmetics/achievements in replay remain read-only until an explicit safe policy is approved; do not promise that new replay discoveries count in the main save. A gallery can replay already-earned cinematics without changing anything. Do not create a realm selector in the fiction.

Proposed save contract: sourceSaveId, snapshotId, selectedBranch, contractRevision, validatedProgressionManifest, historicalQuestLedger, transitionReceipt, destinationCheckpoint, activeContext and recoveryState. Each irreversible transition uses a single unique receipt and validated server-owned reward state. Never expose privileged service actions or trust client-supplied inventory. Keep existing guest-entry policy and restricted backend permissions. A cancel or dialogue skip writes no ending. A failed destination load retries the committed branch from its durable checkpoint; an uncommitted attempt restores the source. A second confirmation returns the original receipt rather than awarding again. Save format migration must preserve legacy DONE and missing-vista annotations from R66.

## 3. Recommended playable entry points
Sacrifice: offer the restored age-eighteen landscape as a playable postgame area, initially the R65 public ridge/trail/rest circuit, with only genuinely authored and surveyed destinations accessible. This scope is a recommendation, not owner confirmation. Historical ruins and graves may be visible where appropriate; no live former-world crossing, resurrection quest or promise of a particular person's restoration. A future expansion can add the actual journey and natural-law constraints.
Refusal: continued exploration is confirmed. Recommend returning control at the immediate fused recovery phase (R65 B1) after the later coda, keeping the playable protagonist eighteen. Clearly label the twenty-year scene as a look ahead, not the active save's present. If the owner instead wants the playable world twenty years later, revise avatar age, NPC lifetimes, equipment appearance and quest availability together; never quietly mix the phases. Only actual surviving and newly authored actors populate the active world.
