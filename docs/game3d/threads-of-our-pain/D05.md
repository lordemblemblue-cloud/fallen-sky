## Scene 1 — The water no animal will drink

A grey wolf stands between the trail and pool, sniffing the water before turning its muzzle away. Its flank rises too quickly. Rather than pursuing the protagonist, it retreats toward a split boulder. The hero lowers his weapon and stops at the dry bank.

Hero (softly): “You don't want me near it, do you?” [Waits, open hand at his side.] “All right. I'll stay here.”
