# S08 — Letters Under the Bridge
R55 | Optional adventure, river crossing. Catalog: scattered courier letters, one describing absent old bridge, older delivers medicine across intact bridge under attack, letter addressed to unknown town, player retains a copy, traversal and optional rescue; D06 optional. All extra details and rewards proposed. D06 owns Courier Belt, satchel and paired alignment; S08 owns letter receipts and rescue.
## Scene 1 — The river took the post
Age fifteen. Fog hangs above the bend. A courier is kneeling on the road beside an overturned bag, squeezing water from a cloth flap. A loose envelope catches against a willow branch. The hero stops when the courier lunges toward the bank, then grips the bag strap to prevent it sliding farther.
Courier (embarrassed, breathless): “Don't worry about the bag. The letters are getting away.”
Hero: “How many?”
Courier (counts empty loops on the flap): “Three I can see. One was addressed to a town nobody knows.”
Hero: “Why were you delivering it?”
Courier: “Because someone wrote an address. Doesn't mean I get to decide it isn't worth finding.”
Player can offer help, ask about the address, or leave and discover the letters independently. No timer; paper drifts within authored catch zones and cannot despawn downriver. If D06 was completed, protagonist may recognize the crossing but cannot use that as proof of the town's history. The courier directs the player toward a high trail, not the missing span.
## Scene 2 — Three pieces of a route
Letter A is caught in willow roots above shallow water. Letter B rests on a dry ledge near a broken pier, reached by either stepped bank or a longer ridge path. Letter C lies in a small maintenance recess behind an old retaining wall, accessible by a visible crawl-free opening. Loose pebbles and creaking timber warn of an unstable shortcut; the wide route is always available. A misstep lands in shallow recovery water or on a lower shelf with ramp, not a lethal current. Each envelope has a distinct seal shape, subtitle cue and high contrast edge; players can use a contextual search assist. Inspect only the address and exposed map sketch; return sealed private letters. At the third envelope a faded line drawing shows a complete bridge where the broken pier stands. The hero turns the paper until the river bend matches.
Hero (almost under his breath): “I know that road.”
Courier (if accompanying): “From a map?”
Hero: “Maybe. I thought there were houses on the other side.”
Courier (looks through the fog): “There's a footpath. Never seen houses.”
Optional rescue: a traveler calls from a low stone shelf after slipping on the wet bank. Player can guide them up the recovery ramp or call the courier to assist. They wait on stable footing, never drown on a timer; refusing does not block letter recovery. Rescue receipt grants local thanks, not a D06 discovery flag.
## Scene 3 — The bridge still stands
Age eighteen. The intact bridge spans the same river bend. Canvas covers a medicine crate at a protected staging point; a defender signals outgoing traffic. Across the bridge a healer waves from a shelter entrance. Distant impacts shake dust from the parapet, with visible hazard zones and intervals. The older hero wears his existing Courier Belt and carries his satchel. He has not recovered new equipment.
Courier (braces the crate with a knee): “The far shelter's running low. The small case is medicine. Leave the large one for the cart.”
Hero: “Can I take the lower passage?”
Defender: “It's open while the middle is blocked. Keep to the marked lane.”
Player carries a lightweight quest case along the protected parapet or waits for a safe interval on the central route. If combat chosen, defend the approach until the crossing clears; no mandatory kills and no reliance on special weapons. Optional rescue of a stranded runner on a refuge platform; use the same safe interval and direct them to the shelter. A failed crossing returns to the last safe refuge with the case intact; no civilian dies because of retry. At delivery the healer opens the case and checks its contents.
Healer (relieved): “That's enough for tonight. Can you set it here?”
Hero: “Who gets it first?”
Healer: “The ones who can't wait. I'll take it from here.”
The hero feels cold fog across his hands although the bridge is dry. He studies the road beyond the shelter, then returns control to the player. The younger hears the rattle of a cart from the empty riverbank. This is an actual playable experience, not a replay; if the original opportunity is unavailable, preserve the S08 younger-world completion and offer only a journal lead, not a fake reenactment.
## Scene 4 — A letter for a place that isn't here
The courier spreads a dry cloth over a stone table on the younger bank. The three envelopes are checked by seals, not opened without permission. The unknown-town letter has a copied route sketch on the outer fold. The courier turns it toward the protagonist.
Courier: “One letter was addressed to a town nobody knows.”
Hero: “I know that road.”
Courier (studies him, not dismissive): “Then keep the copy. If you ever find the place, let me know.”
Choices: keep the copy privately; ask the courier to mark the old route on a public map (only non-private geography); decline the physical copy but record the clue in the journal. All three return the original letters and complete the adventure. If the optional rescue was completed, the traveler brings a dry cloth and thanks the hero; otherwise the scene remains focused on the post. No confirmation that the courier's town existed in this reality, no automatic M07 proof or new main gate. Proposed reward standard XP and small travel supplies, separate rescue thanks; copy is a non-sellable lore item. No Courier Belt or capacity bonus from S08.
