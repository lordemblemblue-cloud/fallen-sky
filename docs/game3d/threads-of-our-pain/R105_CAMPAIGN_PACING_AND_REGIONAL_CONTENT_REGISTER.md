## 3. Organic encounter rules and proposed reveal budget
Use witnessed event, environmental discrepancy, voluntary conversation, actual source-linked clue, or neutral journal lead. Passing an ambient actor is not acceptance or completion. Keep one salient interaction prompt per immediate scene; use an existing journal for deferred leads, not a new persistent HUD or dozens of exclamation marks. Recommended staging target (not hard cap or lock): one primary story direction and one or two nearby visible optional leads in a single approach view, other valid opportunities discoverable by walking or reading. Do not hide an eligible quest solely because a prompt budget is full. Nonintrusive journal annotation when a known irreversible deadline is approaching; only verified actual closures. Never create an arbitrary quest timer. During M05/M10/M19 combat/transition, queue nonessential notifications until safe; do not suppress safety and objective cues. During M19 review, allow Back/cancel and safe exploration where existing rules permit. No reward stinger during loss. Conversation, inspection, rumor and witnessed assistance differ: rumor is not evidence or a completion receipt. Short return acknowledgments when already earned; no repeated explanatory scene or XP.
