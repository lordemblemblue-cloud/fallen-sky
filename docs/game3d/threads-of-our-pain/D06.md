# D06 — The Bridge in the Mist

S08 independent. Distinct from D35's missing mountain span and rope. New staging proposed.

## Scene 1 — The road that reaches nothing
Age fifteen. Afternoon fog hangs above a wide river. Willow leaves brush a muddy road that ends at a broken stone pier. A faded direction post leans toward the water; paper caught in a branch turns in the current. If S08 is active this is a courier letter clue, otherwise ambient dressing. The protagonist lifts a foot as though expecting a paving stone beyond the edge, then steps back.

Hero (frustrated): “I was about to step straight off the road.” [Looks across.] “Why did I think it kept going?”
Ferryman (optional NPC, repairing a rope knot): “Nothing across that stretch but fog. Take the long path upstream.”
Hero: “Was there ever a bridge?”
Ferryman (shrugs): “Old stones don't tell me where they came from.”

The muddy road and dry ridge both approach a stable shelf. The broken pier, curve of the bank and distant arch-shaped rock resolve a bridge sign. The hero hears a brief rattle of wheels. No NPC dialogue or letter required. If S08 completed, preserve its letter copy and retrospective journal context.

## Scene 2 — Keep the middle clear
Age eighteen. The same bend supports an intact masonry-pier and timber-deck bridge. Beyond the ridge an intermittent siege is audible. A courier shelters medicine beneath canvas; a defender waves a cart through. The older hero already wears his Courier Belt and carries his satchel.

Defender (calls without looking away): “One cart at a time. Leave room for people coming back.”
Courier: “Can you carry this one? The far shelter is running low.”
Hero: “How bad is the road?”
Courier (glances across): “Bad enough that I need someone who won't leave the medicine halfway across.”
Hero (tightens his belt): “I'll get it there.”

If S08 active, use its delivery and receipt; otherwise observe a supply transfer or help with an ambient crate, without auto-starting S08. If complete, replacement traffic remains. From protected staging, the intact piers, bend and arch form the matching sign. Attacks remain telegraphed outside the focus area. He hears water striking old stone despite cart noise, pauses, and waves a civilian through. This is an ongoing playable scene, not a flashback.

## Scene 3 — Walking into the fog
Both first flags permit the younger to hold focus on the shelf. Fog parts along the absent span: a parapet outline, then stone paving. The revealed crossing echoes the older bridge but need not be its historical ruin. Collision, navigation and far landing load before visibility. Focus release outside hides the span; once occupied, maintain collision, local visible return lane and refuge until safely off the crossing.

Hero (careful first step): “There it is.” [Beat.] “Where was it?”

White water and loose grit warn of broken edges; the center remains wide and safe. At the far bank a notch behind the arch leads to a river cave. The player can rest, return or enter on foot. Optional glide landing may be surveyed upstream, but cannot skip the two first flags. No disappearing floor or required leap into fog.

## Scene 4 — A bag packed for another road
Mineral bands resemble road markings. A dry shelf overlooks a shallow channel; a bench-like slab holds a courier's satchel. An optional alcove contains a blank wax impression and wooden peg, not a new S08 letter. A lower route crosses a visibly pulsing surge; wait or take the upper shelf. A fall lands on a safe recovery ramp, never in a lethal invisible volume.

Hero (unfastens satchel, traces repaired seam): “I know how this is stitched.” [Adjusts strap without looking.] “It fits.”

In the original shelter the older hero stops while a delivery is checked. He grips the satchel already at his waist and moves his belt buckle a notch, surprised by its familiar weight.
Attendant: “Did the strap break?”
Hero (shakes head): “No. It feels different.”
Attendant: “Good different?”
Hero: “I think so.”

The younger briefly feels a sun-warmed buckle despite the cave chill; the older hears a drop strike stone. The satchel is the recovered object; Courier Belt is the catalog equipment/utility unlock. Capacity tuning TBD, distinct from D35 rope and S08 copy. Older inventory unchanged.

## Scene 5 — More than supplies
An upper overlook frames three rock edges matching bridge deck, far arch and road bend. The second focus reveals the older crossing in use. A defender holds a hand out for a child to wait for a cart; the older sets a crate beside a shelter door to ease a courier's burden.

Courier (rubs shoulder): “You can put it down now.”
Hero: “Where does it go after this?”
Courier: “Inside. Then wherever it's needed.”

The younger studies the far road with difficult recognition. “I crossed this before it broke.” The older feels quiet mist, then looks at the intact span and says nothing. Free camera throughout; optional hold-to-observe. An independent upper path returns via a safe bank trail. Journal records resemblance, not a confirmed reincarnation or explanation.
