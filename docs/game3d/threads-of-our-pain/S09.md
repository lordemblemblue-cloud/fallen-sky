# S09 — The Miner Who Drew a Door
R57 | Source: original S09 catalog, R10 D07 and M07. Twin caves / West Root cliff. The miner's sketch, three rock edges, focus-dependent door and original-world access hatch remain mandatory. D07 owns Miner's Gloves, climbing utility and second vista. This script adds the miner's independent investigation and report, not a second discovery. New dialogue, route survey and rewards are proposals.
## Scene 1 — A drawing somebody laughed at
Age fifteen. At the foot of the West Root cliff, the wind keeps lifting one corner of a charcoal drawing. Its owner has weighted the other corners with chips of sandstone. A second worker is packing a hammer into a cloth bundle; he glances at the drawing and tells the miner that the cliff has no door. The miner does not answer until the worker has left. He has crossed out several measurements and rubbed the paper nearly through where the bottom corner should be. The player encounters the argument by walking the trail; no quest marker appears before the scene is witnessed.
Miner (irritated, guarding the paper): “I drew what I saw, not what was there.”
Hero: “Did you draw it from here?”
Miner (points toward a sheltered ledge): “Up there. By the time I got down, it was gone.”
Choice — Believe him: “Show me where you stood.” Miner: “Mind the loose stones. I don't need another pair of bad knees.” Choice — Ask for evidence: “Can I compare the sketch with the cliff?” Miner: “That's all I've been asking anybody to do.” Choice — Decline: “Maybe later.” Miner folds the sketch and waits at camp; no failure flag.
The hero can take a tracing, not the miner's original. Three visible terrain edges match the sketch from separate positions, but the complete outline is only legible from a stable standing zone. The miner asks the hero to mark the route before anyone attempts to climb the cliff. The initial task is investigation, not proving that a supernatural door exists. The younger believes it may be a trick of the light; the player may already know more through D07.
## Scene 2 — The hatch that opens every day
Age eighteen. At the corresponding supply corridor, the quartermaster has propped a working hatch open with a crate. Rain has darkened the stone supports. A worker checks a manifest, crossing out a damaged load rather than inventing a replacement. The older protagonist already wears his gloves. He lifts the crate clear and holds the hatch while the worker passes. The door frame has the exact outline he saw in charcoal, although he cannot remember where he saw it.
Quartermaster (practical, without looking up): “Leave enough room for the next person.”
Hero (glances at the lower corner): “Has this always been here?”
Quartermaster: “Long as I've worked this road. Why?”
Hero: “Nothing. I thought the bottom edge was broken.”
The player may inspect the frame or assist with the crate; the optional original-world S09 observation does not grant D07 focus unless the actual D07 alignment succeeds. The older protagonist hears a pencil scraping against paper, looks at the manifest, and continues working. He does not name the younger world. Preserve the R10 D07 scene if already played; show a brief maintenance exchange instead of repeating the same crate action.
## Scene 3 — Stand here and look
Back at the cliff, the player places three removable survey pegs on safe ground corresponding to the rock edges. The miner joins the player at the viewpoint, using a walking path rather than jumping. When D07's two focus flags are available, voluntary focus reveals the existing door; use R10's exact exchange once: Hero: “Stand here and look.” Miner: “There it is!” He takes a half-step and stops. He has finally been believed, but he does not rush into the opening. If D07 is already complete, the miner acknowledges the prior discovery and inspects the surveyed approach. If its flags are incomplete, the miner can still verify the three edges from the sketch; no fake door appears and no D07 credit is awarded.
Miner (looking at the ground instead of the opening): “I can mark where to stand. Can't promise what's behind it.”
Hero: “Then mark what you know.”
Optional exploration uses D07's existing locker, shaft shelf or ventilation bypass. The internal latch is the only permanent safe outward passage; no new entrance into the relic room. The player can inspect the latch and bring back an unsigned shift tally rubbing. The miner remains outside. If the player does not have D07 access, survey the exterior trail, identify the dangerous approach, and return a provisional route report. This is a distinct S09 outcome, not completion of D07. No mandatory combat or recovered climbing ability.
## Scene 4 — A useful uncertainty
The miner lays the tracing alongside the original. The player chooses: certify the doorway if genuinely seen and mark the safe route; provide only a provisional exterior survey; or ask the miner to keep the sketch private. The privacy choice records whichever evidence was actually observed, never a false claim. Miner (confirmed): “I'll draw the path in. Not the part I haven't walked.” Miner (provisional): “At least I know where to look tomorrow.” Miner (private): “All right. I'll keep the warning on the trail, not the drawing.” His camp gains a small route notice appropriate to the result. The player can later add a verified doorway/latch observation to a provisional record without a second reward. M07's carving remains a separate clue. The old and young hero share a sensation of knowing the outline, but the miner sees only a local manifestation, not another reality.
