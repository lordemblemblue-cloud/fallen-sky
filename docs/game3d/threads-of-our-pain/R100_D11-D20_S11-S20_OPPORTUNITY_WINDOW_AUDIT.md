## Common opportunity contract
An original-world (O) step requires an actual permitted visit, correct local phase, eligible actors, usable geometry, safe route and the specified performed action or observation. An unsafe or unloaded encounter is temporarily waiting, not necessarily permanently closed. Only an authored closure and absence of a legitimate remaining route establish missed-this-cycle; unknown stays unknown. R97 pre-death local ordering is provisional. Do not translate chapter presentation gates into O calendar dates. For preserved (P) and ending routes validate live actors, actual evidence and safe accessibility. Both endings preserve owned gear; expired historical events may be earned in NG+ with no duplication. No new portal, ordinary NPC migration, resurrection or time selector.
For all paired discoveries, actual first alignments in either supported order, reveal while focused, collision retained until safe clearance, persistent internal egress, unique claim and required independent second alignment after claim. Older already owns/knows the reward; touches existing object or recognizes technique as new without inventory mutation. A second vista depicts a real lived scene, not a new playable original visit or evidence of an optional action the player did not perform. Use only source-authorized baseline variants. Keep claimed rewards if recollection is pending; R66 legacy completion provenance retained. Maintain actual/reported/inferred/unknown and consent boundaries.
