# Bow fix: the string hand stays beside the face (2026-11-28)

The owner's report: "her hand is going behind the and inside her body and inside her head". Eve's string hand did this
while she charged a shot. The owner's recording shows it from the game camera behind her.
- The bow is held up on her left.
- As the charge rises, her right hand crosses to the left side of her face and behind her head.
- The right forearm crosses her upper back.

## Cause

The bow had no clip files.
- **The poses are code.** bowDraw, bowHold, bowRelease and bowQuick are poses written in code in
  `src/components/game3d/weapons/weaponComboClips.js` (`BOW_SHOTS`), and both bodies share them.
- **Too little turn.** Their aim turns the chest only 24°.
- **Wrong side.** Their full-draw target for the string hand sits toward the middle of the face, so on Eve the hand
  ends up on the far side of her head.
- **Measured.** On her own body file, the code-made hold puts the middle knuckle at −0.98 of her head's half-width,
  which is past the centre of her face on the other side. This branch measures +1.17.

## Change

New clips for the weapon clip pack (`public/models/animations/weapons/`). The game already plays a pack clip in place of
the code pose of the same state (`weapons/weaponClipPack.mjs`). No game code changes.

| Body | State | File | Length | Timing (owner, 2026-11-19: unchanged) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eve (`fused-female-v1`) | `bowDraw` | `female/bowDraw.glb` | 0.45 s | nock 0.16, full 0.45, hit 0.45, cancel 0.45 |
| Eve (`fused-female-v1`) | `bowHold` | `female/bowHold.glb` | 1.6 s loop | hit 0, cancel 1.6 |

- **The draw.** The string hand draws along the arrow to an anchor beside the jaw, at the height of the corner of the
  mouth. The hook's fingertips rest on the cheek. The arrow sits on the aim, and the bow arm points along it.
- **The stance.** The pelvis is 30° and the chest 55° off the aim, with the head 8° short of the target. That is
  side-on enough for the anchor and open enough for the game's aim-walk, where the legs walk and the chest keeps the
  clip's facing.
- **Charge levels.** Past the nock the game slows the draw down to charge it. Every frame between the nock and full
  draw is a charge level the player sees, and every frame is checked.
- **Hold to release.** The hold breathes a little and loops seamlessly. Full draw is the hold's first pose.

## How it was made

- **Keyframed in Blender 5.2.3, headless**, in the Atom X Eve Animation Studio, outside this repo (package `bow-game`).
  - It uses the studio archer solver: per-frame inverse kinematics on a copy of the weapon studio.
  - The string hand's middle knuckle is the point the game's string follows.
- **No Cascadeur yet.** Its script server was off, and this was not done in Cascadeur. A pass in the Cascadeur Pro
  Trial would be for internal tests only, because its licence bans commercial use.
- **Licence trail:** Blender only.
- **The owner's lesson video** was used as a visual reference only. Nothing was captured or copied from it.
