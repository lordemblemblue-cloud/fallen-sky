## The First Stroke

Main story, chapter 3. Asked by Kira the Scout.

- **Sworn to:** Kira the Scout, of the Fountain Watch
- **Why it is asked:** Whoever killed Elder Maren used ink that has not dried, and the only ink like it comes off the wall of the first mural.
- **What keeping it costs:** What you find in the socket will point at somebody, and you will have to decide who is told.

**When it is offered**

- **Kira the Scout:** You're the one who chased it. Good. Smell this. It's the ink off Sable's three arrows, and it is still wet. Ordinary ink dries in an hour. This hasn't dried since the day Maren died.
- **Kira the Scout:** Only one place in the valley bleeds ink that stays wet: the Jade Lantern Grotto, far south-west, past the deserters' fires. Master Hesper is down there, renewing the first mural. Ask him what could draw an archer out of a wall.

**Step 2. Go down into the grotto.** *(Jade Lantern Grotto)*
