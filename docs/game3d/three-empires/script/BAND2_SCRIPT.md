## A Farmer's Son in a Blue Coat

Akitsu's story, chapter 1. Asked by Sergeant Gorō.

- **Sworn to:** Sergeant Gorō, at the drill yard of the Western Road camp
- **Why it is asked:** His recruits are farmers who were handed rifles that will not fire here, and they have one evening to learn a spear.

**When it is offered**

**Step 3. Hold the picket against what comes out of the cedars.** *(Camp of the Western Road)*

## The Sword Mistress's Price

Akitsu's story, chapter 2. Asked by Lady Asagiri Tomoe.

- **Sworn to:** Lady Asagiri Tomoe, head of House Asagiri
- **Why it is asked:** Her grandson carried the banner to the Wolf Stones and did not come back, and the conscripts will not be taught until it is home.
- **What keeping it costs:** You carry a dead boy's swords home on foot, and you are the one who hands them to her.

**When it is offered**

- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** You are the one Gorō sent. You hold that blade like a farm tool. Do not apologise. A farm tool feeds people.
- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** My house has one banner. My grandson carried it to the Wolf Stones nine days ago, to ask the mountain whether we still had a place in the world. He was sixteen. The stones kept him.
- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** Bring me the banner and his two swords. Then I will teach your conscripts. Not before. A house that leaves its banner in the dirt has nothing to teach.

**Step 1. Go to the Wind-Carved Wolf Stones.** *(Wind-Carved Wolf Stones)*

- **Ren:** She sent you? She wouldn't let me go. She said one of us was enough to lose. It's there, by the tall stone. The thing holding it wears his face.

**Step 2. Take the banner from the echo that holds it.** *(Wind-Carved Wolf Stones)*

- **Ren:** It's only ink now. He's been gone nine days. I keep telling myself that.

**Step 3. Carry the banner and the two swords to the hall, on foot.** *(House Asagiri's Hall)*

- **Ren:** He was better than me. Faster. He'd have hated that I'm the one left to say so.
- **Ren:** When the court took our stipends, she sold the garden to keep the school. When they banned the swords, she went on teaching with sticks.
- **Ren:** Don't tell her I cried. She knows. Don't tell her anyway.

**Step 4. Pour the water at his grave.**

- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** Slowly. He was never in a hurry unless he was late.

**Step 5. Stand before Lady Asagiri.**

- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** The banner is torn. Good. It was too clean. It had never been anywhere.
- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** Your grip. Here, and here. The cut begins in the hip. Again. Tomorrow at dawn, bring your farmers. Tell them to eat first.

**When it is finished**

- **Lady Asagiri Tomoe:** You did not say you were sorry. Everyone says it. Thank you for not saying it.

## Opening of the Mouth

Tameri's story, chapter 2. Asked by Lector-Priestess Meresankh.

- **Sworn to:** Lector-Priestess Meresankh of the House of Life

- **What keeping it costs:** You have to strike down your own people before you can free them.

**When it is offered**

- **Lector-Priestess Meresankh:** Sit. You carried the grain. Good. Now the other count. Thirty-one of ours have died in this valley. I have opened the mouths of nineteen. The rest are in the ravine, and at night they stand up.
- **Lector-Priestess Meresankh:** A person whose mouth is not opened cannot speak their own name to the judges. They cannot eat. They wander. Here, under this sky, they wander with their bodies.
- **Lector-Priestess Meresankh:** The blade for the rite is sky-iron. A shade took it from my tent three nights ago and carried it to the old battle camp south of here. Bring it back, and bring it to me in the ravine.

**When it is offered**

- **First Centurion Titus Petronius:** Twenty-five years I've walked behind a standard. Today I walk behind a plough, and gods help me, I'm more nervous. Take the handles. I'll lead.

- **First Centurion Titus Petronius:** Straight! A furrow is like a marching line. Nobody remembers the straight ones. Everybody remembers the crooked.

## The Crack

- **Sworn to:** Legate Gaius Vettius Corvinus, commander of the Ninth
- **Why it is asked:** The road from the pass is not safe, and what the courier carries must reach the Legate unseen.
- **What keeping it costs:** You will be trusted with something you were not meant to know.

**When it is offered**

- **Legate Gaius Vettius Corvinus:** Old Vine gave me your name. He does not give names. A courier is coming down the High Road with a case under the Senate's seal. Meet him. Bring him in. Do not open the case. Do not ask about the case.

**Step 1. Meet the courier at the top of the High Road.** *(The High Road)*

- **Marcus Dexter:** The Ninth? Thank the gods. Four days I've ridden, and something has kept pace with me since the pass.

**Step 2. Break the ambush on the road.**

**Step 3. Bring the courier down to Castra Lupa.** *(Castra Lupa)*

**Step 5. The Tribune wants a word.**
