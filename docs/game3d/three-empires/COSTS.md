## The six townspeople, 2026-11-22

The owner approved about 180 credits for the six people who stand in the town during the opening; they cost 180, 30 each, every step right the first time. Each was drawn first and built from the drawing: Sable Vey in a slate-blue coat with her chain of office, Master Hesper with ink to the elbows, the Gate Warden in the Watch's green, Lieutenant Mizuhara Jun in an Akitsu engineer's blue, Paneb the Scribe in pleated linen and a beaded collar, Tribune Aelia Sabina in a leather cuirass with her sword tied shut. Each is one file of 0.81 to 0.93 MB and about 12,500 triangles, one draw call, with Idle, Talk and Walk. With Bram and Elder Maren, all eight named people of the town now have a model of their own.

## Elder Maren, 2026-11-22

The owner approved about 30 to 35 credits; she cost 30. Built from a picture this time, and the picture, the model and the game file all show the same woman: small, white hair in a low bun, a cream tunic, a sage-green shawl crossed and pinned, a cord belt, grey trousers, soft shoes. First try at every step. Packed for the game she is `public/game3d/threeroads/cast/elder_maren.glb`: 0.83 MB, 12,639 triangles, one draw call, clips Idle, Talk and Walk.

## The first test, 2026-11-22

The owner approved 26 credits for one test character, asked for as Elder Maren. The shape came back usable; the colour step turned it into a white-bearded old man in a green vest. Rather than pay again blindly, the same model was finished as **Bram the lamplighter**, whom it fits. Elder Maren was made afterwards, from a picture (below).

What it produced: one mesh of 12,635 triangles, one material, a 24-bone skeleton, and the clips Idle, Talk and Walk. Packed for the game it is `public/game3d/threeroads/cast/bram_lamplighter.glb`, 0.82 MB with a 1024 px texture: one draw call. The shared stand-in figure the cast wears today is four to five draw calls and about 20,000 triangles.
