# Reference longplay: how a normal main-line playthrough really paces

Companion to [REFERENCE_QUEST_MAP_NOTES.md](REFERENCE_QUEST_MAP_NOTES.md). Written 2026-11-27 at the owner's request: "you don't have to use that YouTube link any more, use this one".

**Source.** The owner's own downloaded copy of a no-commentary main-line longplay (YouTube id `p0I7ccItmv8`; the file stays outside the repo; 8.98 GB, 854×480, **21 h 8 min**). Despite the `_003` in its file name it starts at the opening scene and runs to the end credits of the main line.

**For structure only.** Reference places appear only as generic descriptions or lookup labels. Nothing here is proposed as content for our game.

**How it was read.** The file was streamed from a small local web server into the built-in browser (no ffmpeg on this machine; nothing was copied or saved from the video). Frames were sampled **every 10 seconds (7,609 frames)** and measured for brightness, colour and darkness; a thumbnail every 30 seconds (2,537) was laid out on contact sheets and looked at by eye; each of the 45 long indoor stretches was opened at full size and labelled by eye; world-map views were found by matching the map's colour signature and then confirmed at full size.

## 2. The rhythm

- **No single kind of activity lasts longer than ≈30 minutes** (longest indoor stretch 31 min, longest outdoor leg 28 min). The median switch between indoors and outdoors comes every **8–10 minutes**.
- **A door every ≈7 minutes**: entering or leaving a hall, a cave, an inn or a tomb is the basic beat of play.
- **Talk scenes are long.** The ruler's hall takes 12–30 minutes on each visit (2:13–2:43, 2:55–3:20); the peace council takes 17 minutes (19:07–19:24).
- **Dungeons on the main line take 20–40 minutes each** (first tomb 23 min; the relic tomb 36 min; the embassy 37 min). The two big ones take over an hour: the city sewers (≈80 min) and the hunt through a deep ruin and an underground cavern (≈1 h 50 min including travel).
- **The first hour is mostly walking and talking.** Escape 23 min, walk to the village 8 min, village and gear 37 min; the first dungeon only starts at 1:18.
- **Travel becomes a set piece.** The pilgrimage up the great peak took **51 minutes** from the central capital (3:20–4:11), including a 10-minute cave found on the way and an **18-minute climb** up the long stair in snow and wind (3:52–4:10).

**What this shows.** In a main-line playthrough the map **stays mostly blank**. Discoveries form **a band along the roads the story sends you down**; whole regions stay in cloud. Only **1–3 quest targets** are active at any time, and they are usually **in another region**. The player rarely opens the map at all, so the compass and visible landmarks do the guiding.

## 5. Rules this adds for our valley

1. **Plan the main line at real-play length, not speedrun length.** At about 50 minutes per beat, a 12–15-beat main line is **10–12 hours** of play, even in a small valley, because talk, doors and fights do not shrink with the map.
2. **Switch the kind of play every ≈10 minutes and never hold one kind longer than ≈30**: hall → road → cave → road → camp.
3. **A doorway every ≈7 minutes**: our five gates, cave mouths and halls are the beat, so place them for that rhythm along every quest route.
4. **Players navigate by compass and by sight, not by map.** Every quest target needs a landmark visible from the road (smoke, a tower, a banner, a burning wall), and the gates must be readable from a distance.
5. **Keep 1–3 targets active, at least one of them beyond a gate.** That keeps players moving between areas, which is where rival empires will meet.
6. **Discovery follows the story's roads.** Put the optional places within sight of the main routes so they are found on the way, and use them as meeting points for rivals; whatever is far from every story road will rarely be seen.
7. **Make the long walk a set piece once per act.** The pilgrimage climb took 18 minutes and is one of the most memorable parts of the line. In our game, a contested climb or crossing (a bridge, a pass, a stair to a shrine) can be the PvP set piece.
8. **Talk scenes run 10–30 minutes and need a reason to stay**: a council, a choice, rivals present. That is where our empires' players should be able to interrupt or overhear each other.

## 6. Limits

- Labels for beats 15–20 are from single frames and are the least certain (which city, which order of halls).
- The indoor/outdoor split comes from brightness readings, not true scene types: night roads count as indoor, and bright snowfields inside a ruin can count as outdoor.
- The sampling every 10 seconds can miss a world-map view shorter than 10 seconds; the six above were all open long enough to be caught.
- Quest-objective and discovery banners stay on screen only a few seconds, so they were used only where a sampled frame happened to show one, not counted.
- Running speed and distances were not measured from this video; the distances in the earlier notes still stand.
