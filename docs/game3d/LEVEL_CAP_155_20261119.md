### How the rows above 100 were made

The par table was generated by `tools/game3d/stat-report.mjs --write-par`, which isn't in fallen-sky. It assumed
progress = (level − 1) / (cap − 1). Run again at cap 155, it would treat a level-100 hero as 64 % built. That
would lower every row and weaken every enemy from level 1 to 100: a jump for every existing character.

So rows 1–100 stay as measured. Each column above 100 carries on in a straight line at its slope from level 80 to
100, and stops at its build cap:

| Column | Cap | Level where it reaches the cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crit chance | 50 % | about 113 |
| Crit damage | 1 | about 120 |
| Crit defense | 0.6 | already at the cap |
| Armor pen | 20 % | not reached by 155 (15.8 %) |
| Hit bonus | 10 % | already at the cap |

Damage, HP and defense about double from 100 to 155.

I also tried compounding the 50–100 growth. It reached ×3.5 damage at 155, faster than anything levels 101–155
add: 3 attribute points a level and higher gear item levels, both of which grow in a straight line. Enemies would
outgrow heroes.

The comment above the table tells the next person not to regenerate it at cap 155 without the owner.

### Heroes saved at level 100

At the old cap, `xpForLevel(100)` was Infinity, so the game kept adding XP and saved `xpForNext` as null. With the
cap at 155, that pile would buy a level for every 160 XP on the next kill. For example, a hero with 5,000 XP would
go from 100 to 131 in one kill. The Vault flags for review any rise of more than 2 levels plus 6 a minute since
the last save, so that jump would be flagged.

`bankXPAboveCapRaise` handles this when the save loads:
- The hero stays at level 100.
- The bar keeps what fits below level 101, up to 159 of 160.
- The rest goes to `xpBankedAtOldCap` in the save. No XP is lost, and the Character sheet shows the banked amount.
- The next kill gives one level, like any other.
- A save below its cap, or one already saved under the new cap, comes back unchanged.
- Loading the same old save twice banks the same amount, so the Vault's copy arriving later is safe.

The login screen shows the raw save. A hero saved at the old cap reads "Experience not recorded" there until they
enter the world once, which is what it shows today at the cap.

redeploying:

   duel limits.

The private server previews in `server/game3d` aren't deployed; nothing to do.

Order: the functions (1–3) first, then the site. With gear tiers, either order works, but the Legendary items
(level 155, worn from 150) need this branch.

## Limitations

- The rows above 100 are extrapolated, not measured. Real heroes past 100 add attribute points and gear levels,
  but their prestige systems were already at the par's full build at 100. High-level enemies may feel tough until
  the owner answers question 3.
- Rested XP at the cap still works as it did at 100: the bonus is worked out against an Infinity bar. It is
  harmless, because XP at the cap only piles up.
- Multiplayer: until `duelSystem` is redeployed, a duel between heroes past 100 uses the old limits (level 100).
